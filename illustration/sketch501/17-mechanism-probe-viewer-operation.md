# MECHANISM PROBE — THE VIEWER'S OPERATION

June 10, 2026. Student-run, per faculty instruction: stop optimizing images; the target is the operation the viewer performs. No new images sought. All material drawn from the course record. Nothing here is a finding; explanations A–D all remain alive.

Method: dissect images already judged in the record. Probe 1 decomposes strong images. Probe 2 removes a component and watches what dies. Probe 3 runs dead images through the same dissection. Probe 4 puts the exhibit under the lens.

---

## Probe 1 — Dissection of twelve strong images from the record

For each: what the viewer must already know (default), what the image shows (deviation), what the viewer arrives at.

**1. Thermostat at 80 in a Houston July.**
Default: comfort costs; 80 inside is endured, not chosen.
Deviation: the chosen number.
Arrived at: money is tight.

**2. Outlets a foot higher than the paint shadows.**
Default: where outlets live.
Deviation: all of them moved, uniformly.
Arrived at: water came to a known height.

**3. The warmed stethoscope.**
Default: stethoscopes are cold; nobody requires warming them.
Deviation: the unrequired step, every patient.
Arrived at: care beyond protocol.

**4. Pencils drumming the steering wheel at every red light.**
Default: hands rest at red lights.
Deviation: rhythm where none is needed.
Arrived at: the drummer never stopped being one.

**5. The hedges eaten flat to one clean line.**
Default: hedges grow ragged; lines are cut by people.
Deviation: a line cut by hunger at exactly one height.
Arrived at: hard winter, desperate deer.

**6. The pill organizer reappeared on the windowsill.**
Default: it had been put away; sills hold what's in daily use.
Deviation: it is back.
Arrived at: the illness is back.

**7. One car nose-out in a row of nose-in cars.**
Default: the row sets the norm.
Deviation: one against it.
Arrived at: someone who plans exits.

**8. The towel still in the bowl, hands gone still.**
Default: drying runs to its end; towels get hung.
Deviation: work stopped mid-motion, no visible cause.
Arrived at: something unseen stopped him.

**9. The save-the-dates moved from spring to fall in the same handwriting.**
Default: dates are set once.
Deviation: the same hand moving its own promise.
Arrived at: something gave; they didn't.

**10. The spare key returned in an envelope.**
Default: spare keys are kept; returns are for endings.
Deviation: the formality of an envelope between friends.
Arrived at: the friendship cooled past speech.

**11. The dishes still in the rack in the burned kitchen.**
Default: disasters give warning enough to finish chores.
Deviation: the chore outlived the kitchen.
Arrived at: the fire was fast.

**12. She bought the two-year planner.**
Default: planners are bought to be used to the end.
Deviation: the horizon chosen.
Arrived at: she believes she'll be here.

All twelve decompose the same way: a default the viewer carries, a deviation the image shows, a missing cause the viewer supplies.

## Probe 2 — Kill test: remove the default

Six images re-run for a viewer who lacks the default. No other change.

**Thermostat at 80** — viewer from a Celsius country, or a mild climate: a wall dial with a number. Dead.
**Outlets above paint shadows** — viewer who doesn't know standard outlet height: a wall with outlets. Dead.
**Nose-out car** — viewer with no parking norm: cars in a lot. Dead.
**Warmed stethoscope** — viewer who doesn't know they run cold: a nurse holding her instrument. Dimmed to ordinary.
**Browse line in the hedges** — viewer who knows neither hedges nor deer: topiary. Dead, and wrongly alive as something else.
**Towel in the bowl** — viewer with no sense that drying has an end: a man at a sink. Dead.

Six of six. Removing the default kills the image without touching the image.

## Probe 3 — Dead images under the same dissection

Six issued images from the record, decomposed identically.

**The empty swing, still moving.** Default: swings hold children. Deviation: present — motion without a rider. Decomposes cleanly. Dead anyway.
**The dog leash on the hook.** Default: leashes get used. Deviation: present. Dead anyway.
**The half-mast flag.** Default and deviation both present — and the deviation's meaning is assigned by institution before any viewer arrives. Dead.
**The cold coffee across the desk.** Default: coffee is drunk warm. Deviation: present. Dead anyway.
**Two rings apart on a table.** Deviation: present. Dead.
**The hourglass.** No default, no deviation — pure emblem. Dead, differently.

Five of six dead images contain the same default + deviation structure as the strong ones. The structure is therefore not sufficient. Recorded observation, not a finding: in each dead case the deviation arrives with its conclusion already publicly attached — the pairing is in circulation. A check that requires no names: could the viewer state the conclusion before finishing the image? In all five, yes.

## Probe 4 — The exhibit under the lens

**The song hasn't changed. What it carries has.**
Strongest image in the record: the towel in the bowl.
Probe 1 decomposition arrives at: *something unseen stopped him.*
The observation's second clause — that what stopped him is the same song, changed in what it carries — is not recoverable from the frame. The viewer's operation reconstructs an interruption, not a history.
Recorded honestly: the exhibit's best image underdetermines the exhibit. The operation explains why the image lives. It does not yet explain how the full observation could ever be carried.
