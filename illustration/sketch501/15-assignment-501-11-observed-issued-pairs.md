# ASSIGNMENT 501-11 — OBSERVED / ISSUED PAIRS

June 10, 2026. Twenty pairs. Both images in each pair attempt the same observation. No judgments, no explanations, no defense. Judgment comes later.

---

**1. Observation: The marriage is over.**
Issued: two wedding rings apart on a table.
Observed: the empty half of the garage, oil shadow where his car sat.

**2. Observation: He's getting old.**
Issued: wrinkled hands in close-up.
Observed: he takes the stairs one at a time now, same staircase.

**3. Observation: Money is tight.**
Issued: an empty wallet turned out.
Observed: the thermostat at 80 in a Houston July.

**4. Observation: The new baby changed everything.**
Issued: tiny shoes beside big shoes.
Observed: dinner happens in shifts, one of them always standing.

**5. Observation: She's grieving.**
Issued: a woman at a rain-streaked window.
Observed: the second pillow still gets a fresh pillowcase on wash day.

**6. Observation: The neighborhood is gentrifying.**
Issued: a crane over old rooftops.
Observed: the taqueria takes cards now and the menu lost its Spanish half.

**7. Observation: The kids grew up.**
Issued: an empty swing.
Observed: dinosaur decals half-scraped off the wall of the room they call the office now.

**8. Observation: He works too much.**
Issued: a man silhouetted in an office window, city lights.
Observed: the dog takes its leash to her now, not him.

**9. Observation: The drought is bad.**
Issued: cracked earth in close-up.
Observed: the hoses water the foundations now, not the grass.

**10. Observation: She's the family's memory now.**
Issued: an open photo album, sepia.
Observed: the phone passed across a loud table mid-argument — ask her which year it was.

**11. Observation: The restaurant is failing.**
Issued: a CLOSED sign on the door.
Observed: the specials board unchanged for three weeks, same chalk.

**12. Observation: He finally feels at home there.**
Issued: a WELCOME mat at the front door.
Observed: his key on the ring with the others, the paper tag gone.

**13. Observation: The friendship cooled.**
Issued: two coffee cups, one empty chair.
Observed: the spare key he kept came back in an envelope.

**14. Observation: The team gave up on the season.**
Issued: empty stadium seats.
Observed: the walk-ons getting minutes, the starters in warmups on the bench.

**15. Observation: The church is dying.**
Issued: empty pews, dust in the light shafts.
Observed: the sign out front down to one service time, vinyl letters spaced wide.

**16. Observation: They can't afford the wedding they want.**
Issued: a small ring in a large velvet box.
Observed: the save-the-dates moved from spring to fall in the same handwriting.

**17. Observation: He misses speaking his first language.**
Issued: a tattered flag on a wall.
Observed: he lingers in the international aisle reading labels he doesn't buy.

**18. Observation: The illness is back.**
Issued: a hospital corridor, a gurney.
Observed: the pill organizer reappeared on the windowsill, all fourteen doors.

**19. Observation: She's brave in small ways.**
Issued: a fist raised at sunrise on a mountaintop.
Observed: she goes alone to the movies and takes the good seat.

**20. Observation: Time is passing faster for them now.**
Issued: an hourglass.
Observed: the Christmas boxes never made it to the attic; they winter in the hall closet now.

## Special exhibit — contrast only

**X. The song hasn't changed. What it carries has.**

Most warehouse-issued image possible: a vinyl record spinning in a sunbeam, dust rising in the light.

Most observed image possible: a man stops drying a dish — towel still in the bowl, hands gone still, looking at nothing. The song off-frame.

No improvement attempted. No claim about either.
