# ASSIGNMENT 501-2 — ATTEMPTED MURDER OF P-1

June 10, 2026. Ten attempts. Conditions: (1) change primarily in the observer, (2) imagery immediate, (3) image discovered, not assigned. Verdicts honest. No theory, no defense.

---

**1. I salt without tasting now.**

1. Change in observer? Yes — certainty. The pot never knew the difference.
2. Image immediate? Yes — a hand over the pot, no spoon out, no pause.
3. Discovered or assigned? Discovered. It came from the kitchen, not from a library of kitchen images.

Satisfies all three.

**2. I read the last page first now.**

1. Change in observer? Yes — what I need from a book.
2. Image immediate? Yes — a book opened from the back, thumb holding down the unread bulk.
3. Discovered or assigned? Discovered.

Satisfies all three.

**3. I keep my coat on at her house now.**

1. Change in observer? Yes — belonging. The house, the chair, the hostess unchanged.
2. Image immediate? Yes — a seated figure in a coat, mug in hand, everyone else in sleeves.
3. Discovered or assigned? Discovered.

Satisfies all three.

**4. I let the phone ring through dinner now.**

1. Change in observer? Yes — what gets priority.
2. Image immediate? Yes — phone lit and buzzing on the counter, table set in the next room, nobody rising.
3. Discovered or assigned? Discovered, narrowly. The unanswered phone is circulated; the set table and the not-rising are not.

Satisfies all three.

**5. I take the long way past the house.**

1. Change in observer? Yes — the route is a decision, remade daily.
2. Image immediate? Yes — the corner where the detour adds a block, the house held at distance.
3. Discovered or assigned? Discovered.

Satisfies all three.

**6. I can't sit with my back to the door anymore.**

1. Change in observer? Yes.
2. Image immediate? Yes — instantly.
3. Discovered or assigned? Assigned. The image arrived before I finished the sentence, and it arrived from movies. It is exchangeable with every other wary man facing a room.

Fails condition 3.

**7. The dark in the hallway is just dark again.**

1. Change in observer? Yes — the fear left.
2. Image immediate? No. A hallway arrived; the "again" did not. An ordinary dark hallway carries none of it.
3. — (no image to judge.)

Fails condition 2.

**8. I check the lock once now, not five times.**

1. Change in observer? Yes.
2. Image immediate? No. What arrived was the old checking — the hand returning. The change itself, the not-returning, produced nothing.
3. —

Fails condition 2.

**9. My hand finally stopped going to the old light switch.**

1. Change in observer? Yes — a habit extinguished.
2. Image immediate? No. The image that came depicted the former mistake, palm flat on bare wall. The observation is that this stopped.
3. —

Fails condition 2.

**10. I water her plants in silence now.**

1. Change in observer? Yes — the talking stopped.
2. Image immediate? No. A figure watering plants arrived; the silence did not survive the frame.
3. —

Fails condition 2.

---

## Result

Ten were attempted. Five satisfy all three conditions. One failed as assigned. Four failed to image at all.

Ten were not found.
