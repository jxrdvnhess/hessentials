# ASSIGNMENT 501-1 — THE STABLE OBJECT TEST

June 10, 2026. Twelve observations, generated, not searched. Three answers each. No theories.

---

## Six that generated imagery immediately

**1. The ice has gone cloudy in the glass.**

1. What changes? The ice.
2. What stays stable? The glass, the drink, the table.
3. Image immediately? Yes.

**2. The doormat is worn down on one side only.**

1. What changes? The mat's surface, unevenly.
2. What stays stable? The mat, the doorway, the habit of entry.
3. Image immediately? Yes.

**3. The driveway is empty by seven.**

1. What changes? What occupies the driveway.
2. What stays stable? The driveway, the house, the hour.
3. Image immediately? Yes.

**4. She underlines in pencil now, not pen.**

1. What changes? The instrument, and so the mark.
2. What stays stable? The underlining, the books, the reader.
3. Image immediately? Yes.

**5. He sits in her chair now.**

1. What changes? Who occupies the chair.
2. What stays stable? The chair.
3. Image immediately? Yes.

**6. The basil bolted in a week.**

1. What changes? The plant — shape, height, usefulness.
2. What stays stable? The pot, the spot on the counter, the heat outside.
3. Image immediately? Yes.

---

## Six that resisted

**7. The recipe still works. I don't trust it anymore.**

1. What changes? My trust.
2. What stays stable? The recipe, the result it produces.
3. Image immediately? No.

**8. His apologies arrive faster than they used to.**

1. What changes? The interval — the time between offense and apology.
2. What stays stable? The apologies, the two people, the pattern itself.
3. Image immediately? No.

**9. The thermostat reads the same. The house feels colder.**

1. What changes? The feel — the reading my body gives the same number.
2. What stays stable? The house, the temperature, the thermostat.
3. Image immediately? No.

**10. The joke lands somewhere different now.**

1. What changes? Where the joke lands.
2. What stays stable? The joke, word for word.
3. Image immediately? No.

**11. I got better at waiting.**

1. What changes? Me — a capacity.
2. What stays stable? The waiting. The things waited for.
3. Image immediately? No.

**12. Her name costs more to say.**

1. What changes? The cost of saying it.
2. What stays stable? The name.
3. Image immediately? No.
