# 501-13 — The Wall Competition

**Assigned by faculty, 2026-06-11, following the About-page ablation
series.** The wall has survived more attacks than the figure. The wall
carries ~90% of the idea; the figure ~10%. The wall is metaphorical;
the painter is literal — not the same conceptual altitude. The
standing question:

> Is the painter the best answer the wall has faced — or merely the
> first competent explanation that survived contact?

Ten candidates. Their only job: explain why the wall is unfinished.
The painter enters as Candidate A, an incumbent with no privileges.
A candidate wins only by surviving the same contact the painter
survived: the page, the essay, the thirty-second test.

**Family discipline:** no two candidates share an engine. Each is
listed with its answer, its contents, its engine, and its known risk —
the attack it must survive first.

---

## A — THE PAINTER (incumbent)

**Answer:** unfinished because someone is painting it, right now.
**Contents:** figure mid-pull, roller on pole, tray + open can.
**Engine:** present-tense labor; authorship visible.
**Risk:** speaks the language of painting while the wall speaks the
language of discernment — the altitude gap the faculty named.

## B — THE PAUSE

**Answer:** unfinished because the maker stopped mid-stroke to judge.
**Contents:** figure arms-down before the edge, loaded roller resting
on its pole, strip stopped mid-pull.
**Engine:** discernment interrupting production; reader and figure
perform the same act (pause → look).
**Risk:** verdict in its favor arrived carrying the feeling of an
answer (flagged); more melancholy, less warmth than A.

## C — THE DECISION

**Answer:** unfinished because the color is still being chosen — and
choosing well takes time.
**Contents:** no figure. On the bare side, three brushed test swatches
at standing height — clay, sage, limewash (the brand's actual color
studies). The clay field advancing across the wall is the verdict
being acted on. Tray + can remain.
**Engine:** choice among candidates; the wall paints the brand's own
editorial filter. All parts speak the same language: selection.
**Risk:** self-reference — the site depicting its own design process
may read as cleverness. Must survive the Statement test.

## D — THE DAY ENDED

**Answer:** unfinished because daylight ran out; work has a daily

**Engine:** honest limits; aftermath instead of act.
**Risk:** adjacent to the retired room-after family; must not read as

## E — THE PREPARATION

**Answer:** unfinished because most of choosing well happens before

**Contents:** traces only. The bare side carries the work before

**Engine:** the unglamorous majority of the work; future-loaded

**Risk:** spackle dots and tape flirt with the simulated-annotation

## F — THE EDIT

**Answer:** unfinished because the wall is being un-painted — the new

**Contents:** reversal of figure/ground: cream advancing over old
clay; what remains of the clay is what has not yet been edited — or
what stayed.
**Engine:** editing as removal; "this is what stayed" made literal.
**Risk:** inverts the essay's ground (text currently lives ON clay);
the metaphor may collapse into confusion about which side is chosen.

## G — THE INHABITANT

## H — THE NIGHT SHIFT

## I — THE MEASURE

**Engine:** standards precede execution — "the standards stay, the

## J — THE HANDOFF

**Answer:** unfinished because the rest is the reader's.

**Engine:** participation — grammatical conscription (the 401 engine);

**Risk:** one step from cute. The invitation must be discovered, not

## Standing weak observation (faculty, recorded not solved)

The figures that survive longest in memory are slightly lovable —
approachable, not detailed. The current figure is competent, not yet
endearing. Watch: **can a figure become more human without becoming
more detailed?** When the faculty imagines the figure improving, they
imagine more affection, not more realism. Not a finding. Not a task.
An open eye.

## Protocol

Candidates face contact in the same order the painter did: page-scale

incumbency.

## Charm studies (faculty direction after D: more lovable, not more
## detailed — Jansson / Blake / Sempé / Steinberg)

Sheet: samples/charm_studies_sheet.png (charm_studies.py). Five
panels, same stance: v0 rig baseline / v1 economy / v2 storybook
(head +8%, weight ×1.6, feet splayed) / v3 absorption (craned up) /
v4 synthesis.

**Iteration findings, in order paid for:**

1. Economy is not deletion. The first pass merged the raised arm into
   the neck contour and the figure lost its verb. Blake and Sempé
   delete everything except the line that carries the act.
2. Gesture needs air. With a larger head, a fist beside the ear can
   never read; the grip moved out to arm's length and the act
   returned. Spatial clearance, not stroke separation, was the fix.
3. The hypothesis confirmed itself in both directions: charm was
   GAINED where an observed accident was amplified (heel-drift →
   splayed feet, the weighted hip) and LOST where observed detail was
   deleted (hair direction, collar — the bald variants read as
   balloons). Affection lives where observation overrides the rig —
   now survived three independent contacts (faculty's reading of the
   current figure, the deletion test, the restoration test).

**v4 synthesis** (economical body + observed head + crane 0.6 +
weight ×1.6): the warmest figure produced so far, ~20 strokes against
the rig's ~40. STATUS: CANDIDATE, not promoted. Attack required
before any page appearance: survive at page scale (510px), survive
Jordan's eye, and survive beside the working edge without the
softness reading as whimsy.

## Faculty correction of the finding (recorded verbatim in intent)

The mechanism is narrower than "observation overrides the rig." The
successful figures became warmer because they became **less posed** —
the figure that survives is the one that feels least aware of being
observed. The corrected finding:

> The figure became warmer the moment he stopped posing and started
> existing.

Cautions entered with it: the lesson is NOT fewer-lines-better — it
is that **every surviving line must be doing work** (lines describing
generic anatomy go; lines describing specific behavior stay). Study
Sempé/Blake/Jansson for posture and behavior, never for style; the
question is "why does this person feel alive before I can see their
face." Hessentials wants **inhabitants, not characters** — a
character wants attention, an inhabitant gives scale to a world; the
environment stays the protagonist. Final warning: do not manufacture
charm; cute is styling, alive is observation; the brand does not need
a mascot. Figures should feel **briefly encountered** — someone
quietly doing, noticing, deciding, carrying, looking.

Provisional finding (multi-sitting, faculty):

> The strongest figures are not the most detailed or the simplest.
> They are the figures whose posture communicates a specific state of
> attention.

Second finding (faculty): fashion illustration is a useful source of
posture, proportion, and presence — and not a useful source of
aspiration. Study Sempé/Blake/Jansson for posture, behavior,
existence; never style. The figure needs PRESENCE, not charm:
"someone is actually there." Test question for every figure: *if this
person existed, would I enjoy sharing a room with him?* Hessentials
inhabitants should be quietly attractive from behind — pleasant,
comfortable, graceful; posture, neck, shoulders, proportion,
attention — never hairstyle.

## Presence study (v5) — fashion croquis + Sempé reference session

Reference findings (Pinterest: fashion croquis/gesture sheets; Sempé
corpus): the croquis engine is the COUNTERPOSE — shoulder and hip
lines tilt in opposition; the weight ankle lands under the pit of the
neck (settled, not planted); the free knee breaks soft. Sempé: the
spine is always a gentle curve, never a vertical; one decisive
postural fact per figure; identity is silhouette + posture, faces
nearly nothing.

## 501-14 — Five Men Painting The Same Wall (faculty exercise)

Sheet: samples/five_men_painting_sheet.png (five_men_painting.py —
posture as PARAMETERS: bow, slump, head pitch/turn, reach, stance,
knees, heels, free arm, plus engagement distance and whole-body
lean). Six states under the five-man title — all brought; the
absorbed man is v5 unchanged.

Answer to the question (how much of a person can posture alone
carry): far more than expected — six temperaments read from one rig
with nothing added. But the FINDING is in WHICH parameters carried:

> Attention is spatial. Engagement distance and whole-body lean —
> where the body is relative to the work — carried more temperament
> than any body-internal parameter. Slump and reach carried second.
> Head detail (the turn) barely read at all.

The first render failed by timidity: all parameters present, all
amplitudes polite, six identical men. Temperament required
commitment. (Same lesson as the wall's working edge: the difference
must be unmistakable or it is decoration.)

This maps directly onto Sketch 201's core finding — meaning lives in
relationships, not parts. A man's relationship to his work is drawn
with his FEET, not his face. The impatient man leans into the wall;
the master stands back from it; the distracted man's body stays while
his balance leaves. None of that is anatomy.

Faculty corrections logged the same sitting: "pleasant company" →
"he is not performing for us" (the figure appears busy; the reader
observes rather than being addressed). Unit of evaluation is LIFE,
not economy — five more lines for a better posture is a gain; five
fewer that lose the posture is a regression. WORKING HYPOTHESIS
elevated by faculty: "Presence is not created by adding information.
Presence is created when posture reveals attention." Parallel
recorded: the wall became alive when it stopped explaining itself;
the figure became alive when he stopped explaining himself.

Standing watch (open): the hair is arbitrary — a symbol for "head,"
not an observation. The question is not more hair; it is "why is
this THIS person." Unsolved.

## 501-15 — One Man. Five Tuesdays. (faculty exercise)

Sheet: samples/five_tuesdays_sheet.png (five_tuesdays.py; man() in
five_men_painting.py extended with life parameters: waist, hair,
tuck, sleeves, trouser pool). Built as INVARIANTS + SLOW DRIFT, never
variation: same shirt once untucked (a habit, visible by repetition),
same shoes, same wall; monotone drifts — distance from the wall grows
(mastery is spatial, per 501-14), reach economizes, spine bows, waist
thickens, hair thins, trousers pool by year five because they are the
same trousers.

What changed: almost nothing on any single Tuesday; everything across
five. Year one stands close, tucked, working harder than the wall
requires. Year five stands back, hand in pocket, stroke smaller
because none of it is wasted.

**Finding — serial specificity:** the man became THIS person through
the SEQUENCE, not through any single drawing. No panel alone answers
"why is this this person"; the five together do, because the changes
compound and the habits persist. Identity, in this system, is not a
property of an image — it is a property of a series.

**Consequence flagged for the brand (observation, not proposal):**
the site is itself serial — covers monthly, Aurelian weekly,
"standards stay, selections change." An inhabitant could accumulate a
life across the site over real time exactly as this man does across
panels: keep him, let him drift, never reset him. The About painter,
if he stays, should age. The wall he painted should someday be a
painted wall, and he should be doing something else in front of it.

**Honest limits:** the hair-thinning and tuck read subtly at sheet
scale; year one's lean is the most designed-feeling panel (the
least-designed test catches it). And the system still cannot produce
the singular biographical detail — the haircut someone gave him. It
can accumulate plausible evidence; it cannot yet remember. That stays
on the watch with the hair.

v5 (presence_figure in charm_studies.py): v4's economy and observed
details, re-weighted — high working shoulder against dropped free
shoulder against high weight hip; the long right line runs neck →
shoulder → back → hip in one breath; weight ankle under the neck pit;
free heel a breath off the ground. Sheet: samples/presence_sheet.png
(v0 / v4 / v5). Page contact: samples/presence_v5_page_contact.png —
the settle and absorption survive 510px (postural, so they carry).
By the room-sharing test, v5 is the first figure I would call
pleasant company. STATUS: candidate, strongest yet; gates remain
Jordan's eye and the whimsy/mascot watch.
