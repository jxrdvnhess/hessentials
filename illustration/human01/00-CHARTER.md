# HUMAN STUDIES — VOLUME 1

Opened June 11, 2026. Runs beside Sketch 501; replaces nothing.

One question: can you draw human beings well?

Out of scope: symbolism, editorial meaning, doctrine, environments, stories, Hessentials.

## Standard

Not stylization, realism, fashion illustration, caricature, or animation. Competence.
A head must feel alive, observed, physically believable — weight, structure,
individuality, emotion when present.

## Assignments

1. One hundred heads
2. One hundred hands
3. One hundred standing figures
4. One hundred seated figures
5. One hundred walking figures
6. One hundred attractive men
7. One hundred attractive women
8. One hundred emotional faces

## Method

Observe, reconstruct, never trace. Reference: models.com (varied bone structure,
trained faces), Jordan's Instagram, and wherever research leads. No default face.
No Claude face. Line is the carrier; construction underneath, living line on top.

Cadence: run until quality drops. Stop when heads start converging.

## Evaluation

Does this feel like a real human being? →
Does this feel like a specific human being? →
Could this only be this human being?

observation cannot be delegated · identity lives in relationships

broke.
