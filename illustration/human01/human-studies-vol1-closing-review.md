# HUMAN STUDIES VOL. 1 — CLOSING REVIEW
## 2026-06-12. No new observations, no new corpus, no drawings.
## The objective: discover what remains after the volume is removed.

---

## 0 — THE DEFENSE

The faculty asked why "an event stays human exactly as long as the
page keeps both the attention and its receiver" outranks four
rivals. The answer is in four tests, applied to all five candidates.

THE TEST OF PROSPECT. Every rival earned its place retrospectively:
identity-through-relationships was formulated after heads improved;
distributed recognition was formulated after RT-02 surprised the
volume; the carrier classes were formulated after the 2A audit
sorted the wreckage. The attention-receiver discovery is the only
one that was written down as five sealed predictions BEFORE its
test and confirmed 5/5 (reduction_2c.py, pre-registration block).
The others were explanations. This one was a bet that paid. A
discovery that predicts is stronger than a discovery that accounts.

THE TEST OF SCOPE WITHOUT RIDER. Identity-through-relationships and
distributed recognition both carry the permanent conditionality
rider (observer × subject × candidate set) because their judge was
one man judging his own family; the stranger-lineup that would
discharge the rider was suspended. The attention-receiver finding
was judged against the page by the stranger-inference standard —
the only judge the volume could simulate honestly — and it held
across five different events in three different carriers. It is
the volume's least conditional result.

THE TEST OF GENERATION. Ask of each candidate: if it alone
survived, how much of the volume regrows? "Observation cannot be
delegated" regrows the METHOD but no anatomy — it leaves a
disciplined illustrator at zero. The carrier classes regrow a
taxonomy but not the reason some carriers carry — they sort, they
do not explain. Identity-through-relationships regrows faces but
nothing between people. The attention-receiver pair regrows all of
them, because each is the pair at one scale: a face is features
attending to each other at fixed distances (which is exactly what
"identity lives in relationships" says); a carrier is wherever an
attention-receiver pair happens to live (which is what the classes
sort); and delegation fails because the default human — h086
through h094, the confessed nine — is precisely a face with no
attending in it. The template distributed marks; it could not
distribute attention. The volume's oldest failure is this
discovery's negative print.

THE TEST OF DOMAIN. The rivals are findings about humans or about
method. The attention-receiver finding is the volume's only result
about ILLUSTRATION itself — about what a page can keep. Human
Studies is not a psychology course. The page that survives the
fire should be a page about the discipline the volume actually
practices.

The defense rests there, with one honesty: "observation cannot be
delegated" came second by a margin smaller than the inventory
implied, and if the volume were a course in seeing rather than a
course in drawing, the order would reverse.

## 1 — WHAT HUMAN STUDIES TAUGHT ABOUT IDENTITY

FINDINGS. Identity lives in relationships between features, not in
features (RT-01 4/4; RT-02 4/4 with the primary channel withheld —
including a faceless figure at distance, recognized by stance,
named instantly). Recognition is distributed across systems and
degrades as a dimmer, not a switch; total removal produces
collapse ("nobody"), which is a different failure from
misidentification (RT-03, 6/6 pre-registered). Architecture is
per-subject — distributed, concentrated, redundant, conditional —
and only the relationships doctrine transfers between subjects;
feature-anchors are subject-local (the Daniel test, where the nose
failed to travel). Age moves the frame and identity survives
inside it (Jim's grin at 33 and 60; Jennifer's smile across three
generations). Identity survives every emotional state the corpus
produced.

LIMITS, equally taught. Every recognition result carries the rider:
one judge, his own family, candidate sets he chose. The Jensen
Problem showed the judge's exposure history is a variable inside
the result. And the kitchen clip showed identity can be defeated
by register alone — glasses, task absorption, no address — in an
observer who had drawn that face dozens of times. Identity is not
only in the subject; part of it is in the observer's baseline,
which is why the over-stillness drawing (f209) was honest and
inaccessible at once.

NOT FINDINGS, and marked as such: that there is one canonical head
per person (h031 was a convenience of production, never tested);
that the eye region is THE channel (killed by RT-02, preserved in
Section 2 of the inventory).

## 2 — WHAT HUMAN STUDIES TAUGHT ABOUT EMOTION

FINDINGS. Emotion is redistribution, not addition — observed
states moved the face's existing budget rather than spending new
marks, and identity held through all of them (Session 6; the
evidence-line requirement). People are most themselves at the
edges of their states; the peaks belong to symbols — and edited
media cannot show edges because cuts land on peaks, which the
podcast arrival proved from the other side: the edit returned to
the receiver already launched. Emotion is transitions, not states
— a still hides the mechanics a sequence reveals; this was the
assignment's founding amendment and thirty-two transitions never
contradicted it.

SIGHTINGS, fenced: gravity = suppression (the gravest moment
produced the smallest movement — two subjects, two emotions);
gaze leads and the head follows; hands move before faces;
expression and articulation are separable systems (the homilist's
face emptied while his speech never paused); the eyes are the last
to leave a laugh. Recurring, useful, unpromoted.

THE HOLE, stated plainly: worry was never observed. One hundred
and five heads and thirty-two transitions, and the volume's oldest
want is still hypothetical — possibly because every subject in
the corpus knows the camera, and worry may be the state
camera-awareness suppresses most. The volume's warmest property
may structurally exclude its oldest debt. That is a question, not
a finding, and it transfers to Vol. 2.

## 3 — WHAT HUMAN STUDIES TAUGHT ABOUT OBSERVATION

FINDINGS. Observation cannot be delegated — the parametric helper
built from the volume's own measurements resurrected the default
face nine times, and no template has entered the repo since. Time
defeats assumption — the WWHL frames showed nerves as
over-stillness where assumption would have drawn fidgeting. The
provenance of footage is part of the observation: edited media
gives arrivals to the editor; only unedited soil grows true
transitions (Walk 08, method note). And pre-registration converts
observation into research: the two results the faculty accepted as
research — RT-03 and the reduction test — are exactly the two that
were bet in writing before they were run.

DISCIPLINE THAT HELD. The locked recording format (what changed
first / second / last; what returned first / last; later: what
arrived; what moved; where did the information live) kept theory
out of the evidence for thirty-two entries. The count was held
honestly at one when the infrastructure blocked collection, and
closed at thirty when the faculty suspended the gate — never
padded in either direction. The sighting fence kept eleven
recurring observations out of doctrine. Randomness proved a
feature: the uncurated batch outproduced the hunted one, and the
faculty's prediction — that the empty-looking thumbnails held the
yield — was confirmed by the kitchen, the porch, the bed.

THE OBSERVER IS A VARIABLE. Twice the volume learned this on
itself: the conditionality rider on every recognition claim, and
the kitchen miss, where my own recognition failed against a known
face in an unknown register. Vol. 2 should assume the observer is
always inside the result.

## 4 — WHAT HUMAN STUDIES TAUGHT ABOUT ILLUSTRATION

FINDINGS. Carrier precedes composition: ask where the information
lives before choosing what to draw — faces carry only
region-contradiction (the quiet mouth under laughing eyes);
time-properties die on every page; second bodies and baselines
cannot be recovered from a single face (2A audit, 2/5/3,
stranger-standard). The same observation dies in the wrong carrier
and survives in the right one — proven in both directions, f206 to
f211 and back down again in r5. Human events have structural
floors, and the floor is the attention plus its receiver (2C,
5/5); below the floor the drawing is still a drawing but the event
is gone — a bowl with an egg, two balloons, a railing. A floor is
anatomy, not style: the faculty's warning is logged and the volume
repeats it here as its own.

CRAFT, named and bounded. The one known trade of time into a still
is residue — unequal decay between regions (the laugh leaving;
whether residue generalizes is open question 3.2). The occlusion
law held from Assignment 1 through 2C: a thing owns an edge or it
does not exist. The minimum hand is an oval plus fingers and
nothing less — a working hypothesis carved out of four failures,
two foreign and two of my own. And the page audits the corpus:
every drawing that wavered, wavered where its entry was thinnest.
Translation is not downstream of observation; it is observation's
final test.

PREFERENCES, declared as preferences: line over tone is the
volume's hand, not a finding; economy is a discipline the
reductions sharpened, not a law the floors license.

## 5 — WHAT SHOULD SURVIVE INTO HUMAN STUDIES VOL. 2

THE STANDING QUESTION, unchanged: what happened, and where did the
information live. It cost nothing and changed everything
downstream of it.

THE FENCE. The finding / sighting / failure / debt / question
taxonomy, and the verification scoreboard behind it. Vol. 2 should
arrive already knowing that familiarity is not evidence.

PRE-REGISTRATION as the default test format. The volume's two
accepted pieces of research were both sealed bets. Nothing else
produced that grade of evidence.

THE FLOORS, carried as anatomy. Hold 3, kiss 4, between 5, embrace
6, greeting 6 — not as numbers to worship but as the proof that
floors exist and can be found by autopsy.

THE NAMED FAILURES, as entry points: the hand corpus (Assignment
3) inherits the foreign-hand failure and the oval-plus-fingers
hypothesis; the time-carried list (order, modulation, alternation,
baseline) inherits the residue question; the worried edge inherits
the suspicion that this corpus cannot pay it and Vol. 2 may need
different soil — strangers, unguarded rooms, or the patience to
outlast camera-awareness.

THE DEBTS THAT TRANSFER, unretired: nine template redraws, the
RT-04 verdict, news landing on a face. And the rider, attached to
every recognition claim until stranger judges exist.

THE WARNING, last because it should be read last: do not become a
minimalist. The reductions found which lines matter; Vol. 2's
strongest drawings may be the fullest ones the volume has ever
made, provided every line still answers the only question that
survived the fire — what work does this do, and for whose
attention, received where.

---

What remains after the volume is removed: a way of asking where
information lives, a way of testing answers by sealed prediction,
a fence that keeps familiarity out of doctrine, five floors found
by autopsy, four named failures worth more than most successes,
and one page — attention and its receiver — from which the rest
regrows. The volume submits itself for graduation review.
