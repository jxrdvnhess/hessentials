# HUMAN STUDIES VOL. 1 — MIDVOLUME FINDINGS
## Inventory, 2026-06-12. No production. Accounting only.

Status markers used below: a FINDING survived an attack designed
to kill it. A SIGHTING recurs but has not been attacked. The
distinction is load-bearing in this volume and is preserved here.

## SECTION 1 — FINDINGS THAT SURVIVED

### 1.1 Identity lives in relationships, not features.
EVIDENCE: Recognition Test 01 (4/4, unlabeled roster heads in new
states); Session 9 application (Gisele and Douta redraws improved
purely by reallocating line budget to anchor relationships); the
Daniel transfer test (the relationships doctrine transferred to a
new subject when the feature-anchor did not).
SURVIVED: RT-02, the eyes-withheld attack — all four roster heads
recognized with the primary channel removed, including a faceless
figure at distance, named second, instantly.

### 1.2 Recognition is distributed across systems and degrades as
a dimmer, not a switch.
EVIDENCE: RT-02; RT-03 degradation ladder, six positions, 6/6
pre-registered predictions held; the all-removed head produced
identity COLLAPSE ("nobody"), a different failure than
misidentification.
SURVIVED: the ladder itself, which was designed as the kill
experiment for the single-channel thesis.

### 1.3 Recognition architecture is per-subject: distributed
(Jordan), concentrated (Jennifer), redundant (Jim), conditional
(Jensen).
EVIDENCE: the anchor hierarchies and removal tests of 04-anchor-
hierarchy.md; the Jensen Problem analysis.
SURVIVED: cross-subject comparison. CARRIES A PERMANENT RIDER:
every architecture claim is conditional on observer × subject ×
candidate set; the stranger-judge test that would discharge the
rider was suspended with the verification phase and the rider
therefore stays attached to everything in 1.1–1.3.

### 1.4 Observation cannot be delegated.
EVIDENCE: heads h086–h094 — a parametric helper, built from the
volume's own measurements, resurrected the default face nine
times in one batch; confessed in the Assignment 1 ledger.
SURVIVED: its own violation. The finding's strongest evidence is
the failure produced by ignoring it. It has held through every
batch since (every 2A/2B/2C head hand-built; no template has
re-entered the repo).

### 1.5 Emotion is redistribution, not addition; identity
survives every state.
EVIDENCE: Session 6 emotional heads; the per-head evidence-line
requirement; Fifty Faces corpus — no observed state ever
displaced a subject's anchors.
SURVIVED: emotional production at scale (the states changed; the
people stayed recognizable).

### 1.6 The edges rule: people are most themselves at the edges
of their states, and edited media cannot show edges because cuts
land on peaks.
EVIDENCE: the WWHL arc (the volume's first motion observation);
32 collected transitions, all recorded at edges; the podcast
edit-arrival method note (the edit goes TO the stimulus during
the landing and returns to the receiver already launched);
montage disqualified as a form on the same grounds.
SURVIVED: every attempt to harvest peaks or edited footage,
which failed exactly as the rule predicts.

### 1.7 Carrier classes: information is face-carried only when
two regions of one face contradict each other; time-properties
die on the page; sequence, second bodies, and baselines are
face-insufficient.
EVIDENCE: the 2A translation audit (2 A / 5 B / 3 C, judged from
the page); the f211 retest (the kiss that died alone survived
when given its second body); the r5 reduction (the embrace
re-killed on schedule by removing the torso).
SURVIVED: retesting in both directions — a C-class observation
rescued by changing carrier, and a rescued observation re-killed
by removing the carrier.

### 1.8 Human events have structural floors, and the floor is
attention plus whatever receives it.
EVIDENCE: Assignment 2C — five events reduced to death with kills
PRE-REGISTERED before rendering; 5/5 predictions held; floors
observed (hold 3 strokes, kiss 4, between 5, embrace 6, greeting
6); in all five cases the kill was loss of the attending element
or the receiving body, never loss of contact, object, or gesture.
SURVIVED: pre-registration — the volume bet in writing and the
page paid.
FACULTY ACKNOWLEDGED, with standing warning attached: a floor is
anatomy, not style.

### 1.9 Age moves the frame; identity survives inside it.
EVIDENCE: Session 5 — Jim's grin identical at 33 and 60;
Jennifer's smile structure across three generations.
SURVIVED: the elder sessions, and the retirement of the claim it
replaced (see 2.1).

### SIGHTINGS ON THE RECORD — recurring, protected, unpromoted
by design. Listed so the inventory knows they are alive, not
because they are findings: gaze leads, head follows; hands move
before faces; gravity = suppression (two subjects, two emotions);
brow-as-punctuation (three sightings, one subject); expression
and articulation are separable systems; smile-before-the-look;
the eyes are the last to leave a laugh; barbershop eye-parking
under received touch; the verdict pause (three sightings, all
low-stakes); one channel pauses to fund another (CONTESTED — held
under the umbrella, failed on the trail; both on the record).

## SECTION 2 — FINDINGS THAT FAILED

### 2.1 Age = interior mark count.
WHY IT FAILED: one crease aged a face thirty years, but Session 5
showed elders recognizable through proportion shifts with few
marks, and children requiring frame changes (eyes below midline)
no mark count produces.
REPLACED BY: 1.9 — age moves the frame; identity survives inside
it. Mark count remains a TOOL for youth (leave marks out), not a
theory of age.

### 2.2 The nose as a portable anchor.
WHY IT FAILED: Jordan's anchor #2 did not transfer to Daniel
Radcliffe — the transfer test showed feature-anchors are
subject-local.
REPLACED BY: per-subject anchor hierarchies (1.3); only the
relationships doctrine travels.

### 2.3 The climate thesis, v1 (brow-gaze as universal fixed
setting; expression as weather over it).
WHY IT FAILED: Daniel's eye region carries identity as a
characteristic dynamic RANGE, not a fixed setting; one
formulation could not hold both subjects.
REPLACED BY: the reformulated thesis — fixed setting (Jordan) vs
characteristic range (Daniel), both eye-region — itself still
carrying the third-subject falsifier, unrun.

### 2.4 The eye-region as THE recognition channel.
WHY IT FAILED: RT-02 withheld the eyes and recognition survived
4/4. The thesis was, in the professor's words at the time, too
successful — and the attack proved it partial.
REPLACED BY: distributed identity (1.2), with the eyes primary
but not exclusive.

### 2.5 "The smile that arrived before the gaze" as a face
observation.
WHY IT FAILED: the 2A audit showed the still holds both terminal
states while the observation was the ORDER between them — it was
a timing observation passing through a face.
REPLACED BY: the carrier classes (1.7). The entry stands in the
transition ledger; only its classification changed.

### 2.6 The kitchen clip as a recognition failure.
WHY IT FAILED (as first read): I filed my failure to recognize
Jordan in glasses as a conditionality datum. The faculty reread
it as a neutrality success — the first extended observation of a
face not presenting itself to anyone.
REPLACED BY: both readings held jointly, the second now primary;
the open question it spawned (was the subject performing for
anyone?) lives in Section 3.

## SECTION 3 — OPEN QUESTIONS

3.1 Why do foreign hands fail? Two attempts, two failures — and
in 2C my OWN figure's palms decayed toward ears the moment their
finger ticks left. Is the failure "other people's hands," or
"hands without fingers," or "appendages without an owner's
context"? Unanswered until a hand corpus exists.

3.2 Can a time-carried event ever survive a still image? One
partial yes is on the record: the leaving laugh survived as
RESIDUE (the eye-crease outlasting the mouth). Is residue a
general mechanism — can sequence always be traded for unequal
decay between regions — or was the laugh a lucky case?

3.3 Is over-stillness real? Two sightings, two subjects, two
emotions, both reading "gravity = suppression." It needs a third
subject and an honest hunt for a counterexample — a grave moment
that produced MORE motion — before it is anything but familiar.

3.4 Steps versus modulation: the on-camera listener's smile moved
in discrete steps; the off-camera listener's modulated
continuously. Camera's doing, or individual difference? One pair
is not an answer.

3.5 What is the minimum receiving BODY? The embrace floor needed
a torso at six strokes. Is the torso the smallest thing an arm
can mean something against, or is there a smaller receiver?

3.6 Which recognition channels fail first under degradation —
the attack RT-03 was built to set up but the verification
suspension left unrun?

3.7 Does anchor dominance survive stranger judges, or is the
entire architecture catalog (1.3) an artifact of one judge's
exposure history? The Jensen Problem says exposure matters; the
two-judge lineup that would answer it is suspended.

3.8 Does the verdict pause scale with stakes? Three sightings,
all domestic and low-stakes. Unobserved anywhere a verdict
costs something.

3.9 Is shared amusement ever simultaneous, or always
call-and-response at some timescale? The tease (i06) was
sequential and the page falsified it by making it simultaneous;
the couch modulation tracked a running stimulus. No observed
case yet of two faces arriving at amusement together.

3.10 Can the worried edge be observed in THIS corpus at all?
Every subject in the personal archive knows the camera. Worry
may be the state camera-awareness suppresses most — the corpus's
warmest property (repeated, willing subjects) may structurally
exclude its oldest want.

3.11 Was the kitchen subject performing for anyone? The
faculty's question, held open: the camera was running and he
started it. Task absorption with a camera present may be its own
register — neither performance nor its absence.

## SECTION 5 — THE STRONGEST DISCOVERY

**An event stays human exactly as long as the page keeps both
the attention and its receiver; everything else is negotiable.**

Why this one. It is the only discovery in the volume that was
predicted in writing before it was tested, executed five times,
and confirmed five times — the volume's one full act of research
rather than accumulation. And it is the only one the others fold
into without forcing. Identity-through-relationships is this
discovery inside a single face: an eye is nothing, an eye at its
distance from a brow is someone — features attending to each
other. The carrier classes are this discovery sorted by where
the attention-receiver pair happens to live: in one face's
regions, in a body, between two people. The interaction batch
proved it between people — the cradle held at three strokes
because enclosure attended and the small head received; the
blank head killed the between because reception left the page.
The reduction test proved it by subtraction, which is the only
direction of proof a drawing offers. If every notebook burned
except one page, this is the page, because the volume could
regrow the rest from it: start from attention and its receiver,
and the heads, the anchors, the carriers, and the floors are all
recoverable as the places that pair can live.

What this volume knows, in one breath: faces are one of several
places where human information lives; drawings keep events
human by keeping attention and its receiver; everything the
volume once believed beyond that has either survived an attack,
failed one, or is still waiting honestly in line.
