# Sketch 201 · Unit 5 · A36 — The Structure That Returns

Reverse of A35. Begin at **V5** — pure structure: a base of support, a centre of gravity, a
direction. Add exactly **one** thing back, five ways, and watch what *starts* the calf returning.
Image: `loomis/sketch201_u5_a36_structure_returns.png` (generator `_a36_structure_returns.py`).
Run, not predicted.

The five additions (each = V5 + one): the **back-arc** (one contour), the **hump** (one deviation),
a **long-low mass** (one proportion), the **folded foreleg** (one contour), a **head & ear** (one
relationship).

---

**1. Which addition produced the strongest return?** The **back-arc**. Base + centre of gravity +
that single topline sweep, and a reclining body snaps back into view — you read an animal lying
down. The carrying contour does the most, because it relates front to rear over the base; it
rebuilds the *frame* the rest would hang on. Close behind: the **long-low mass** (proportion),
which returns "a reclining bulk" — a body, if not yet an animal.

**2. Which produced the weakest?** The **hump** — by a wide margin. Alone, on the base, it reads as
a small caret on nothing; almost no calf returns. The very mark that carried the zebu identity in
A33 carries close to nothing here. The reason is visible: there is no topline for it to sit on, so
it is not a hump — it is just a bump in space. (The folded foreleg and the floating head & ear were
middling: each returns a *fragment* — a limb, a head — that implies an animal only faintly, because
neither connects to a body.)

**3. What kind of information returns first?** **Relational / structural.** The arc (front-to-rear
over the base) and the body-proportion bring back the *category* — "a reclining body" — before any
individual marker appears. The individuating deviation returns last, and only once it has a
structure to attach to.

---

**The reciprocal finding (paired with A35).** A35 showed structure *survives* after identity is
stripped. A36 shows structure *begins the return* when identity is rebuilt. In both directions the
structure is primary: it is the last to leave and the first to come back. And the deviation is
revealed as genuinely dependent — it needs a structure both to mean something (A33/A34) and to
return at all (here). A hump is only a hump relative to a back. (Said of what these five additions
showed for this calf — I'm leaving it there, not promoting it to a law about all images.)

I will also note, against my own habit: the head & ear addition returned *more* than I'd have
guessed and the foreleg *less* — which is why the experiment is run rather than predicted.
