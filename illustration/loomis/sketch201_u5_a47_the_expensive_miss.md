3. *Distance:* held constant by design — equal gaps cost unequally, so the differences on this page
are not produced by gap size. (Whether bigger gaps cost more, this page cannot say.)

## Withheld

This revises A46's guess. The muzzle was not expensive only because the head draws attention — the
hump break is nearly as costly and sits nowhere near peak attention. What the two expensive breaks
share on this page is sole attachment, not attention. But "redundancy prices the break" is one page
old, attention and identity remain partially entangled, and mass-vs-line (the muzzle is filled, the
hump is a line) is a third variable this page did not separate. The hierarchy is established. Its
explanation is not.
