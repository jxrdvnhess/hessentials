# Sketch 201 · Unit 5 · A37 — The Wrong Return

From V5 (pure structure), add the thing that *feels* most promising — what a novice reaches for.
Five wrong returns: a detailed **face**, **spots**, **horns**, a **tail**, **surface texture/fur**.
Image: `loomis/sketch201_u5_a37_wrong_return.png` (generator `_a37_wrong_return.py`). Compared by
information, not by looks. The goal is to find why the tempting path tempts.

## What the sheet shows

Every one of the five reads *instantly* as cow-or-animal-related — the spots most of all, then the
face, then horns and tail. None of them restores the calf. Each is a cow-signifier hovering over a
base with a centre of gravity and nothing reclining beneath it: a floating face, four scattered
patches, a lone pair of horns, a disembodied tail, a haze of fur over empty ground.

## Why they feel so convincing

- These are the **highest-recognition marks** — the cultural shorthand for "cow." Spots, a face,
horns trigger the *word* immediately.
- **Recognition feels like understanding.** "I can tell it's a cow" gets mistaken for "I have drawn
the cow." The naming is instant, so it feels like arrival.
- They are the most **self-contained and drawable** in isolation — a spot, a horn, a face are each a
finished little icon, so adding them feels like fast, visible progress.
- They look **decorated / finished** quickly, which flatters the effort.

## Why they fail

Set against A36: the back-arc carried *no* cow-signifiers and brought back a reclining body; the
spots and face shout "cow" and bring back no body at all. The difference is the kind of information.
These five add **naming** information — *what kind of thing* — and add **no organizing** information —
*how the thing exists, as a coherent body in this pose, over this base*. With no structure to attach
to, the signifiers float; they label an absence.

- **What they can restore:** the category-name. The viewer says "cow."
- **What they cannot restore:** the reclining body, the weight, the balance, the pose, the gesture —
the relationships A36 showed are what actually bring the animal back. A spot has no pose; a face has
no body; horns hold up nothing.

## The discovery

The tempting path is tempting because it spends on **naming**, and naming is fast, iconic, and feels
like recognition. But a drawn identity is built from **organizing** information, which these marks
cannot supply. Saying the word "cow" is not drawing the animal. (This rhymes with A28's dangerous
illustration and A34's misleading detail; it is also the brand's refusal to signify, met from the
drawing side.) Stated for this calf and these five additions — not pushed into a law.
