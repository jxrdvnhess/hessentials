## What the looking found (not for drawing — for seeing)

Ten people, and the differences are **structural**, not settings on a dial:

- jose is built on a forward belly and a short thick neck, head sunk between broad shoulders — the bulk sits low and leads.
- talha is a long vertical column: symmetrical shoulder slope, no waist, head plumb over the chest.
- linkedin lives on a diagonal — hips behind, head thrust ahead, spine a forward arc.
- zachary's identity is the lean and the folded-arm block, the whole axis tipped off plumb onto one hip.
- wassim and nima both carry the head forward and down, but one is inflated by a puffer and the other rounds toward a railing.
- bruno's whole truth is the inversion — narrow gathered top over a vast lower body, widest at the thighs.
- cemrecan and cole and omar are the slim uprights, and even they split: soft beard-weighted, distant and light, athletic-tapered.

None of those is a "shoulder-width value." They are relationships — what sits ahead of what, what tips toward what, where the mass collects. That is the landmark layer the rig could not reach, because the rig starts from *human* and edits; these start from *this person*. 250 observations, no marks.
