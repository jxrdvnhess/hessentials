# Sketch 201 · Unit 5 · A35 — The Structure Without The Cow

The same calf, reduced to structure only — the carrying arc, the weight relationship (mass low
and toward the rear), the balance relationship (centre of gravity over a wide base), the
directional flow. No ears, dewlap, muzzle, or anatomy. Five versions, each stripping more
animal-specific information. Image: `loomis/sketch201_u5_a35_structure_without_cow.png`
(generator `_a35_structure_without_cow.py`). The question was run, not assumed.

**V1** — the arc in the calf's own contour (the hump, the head and rump ends) over the low
weight-mass, the base, and the flow sweep.
**V2** — the hump removed: a generic resting topline.
**V3** — the topline reduced to a single reclining sweep over the low mass.
**V4** — flow and balance only: a gesture over a base.
**V5** — a direction and a centre of gravity over a base: pure structure.

---

**1. When does the calf stop being identifiable?** As *this* calf, at **V2** — the moment the
hump is removed, the zebu reading goes (the A33 finding, confirmed from the other side). As *a
cow / a resting animal* at all, around **V3→V4**: the single sweep still reads as a reclining
something, but once it drops to a flow line over a base, the animal is gone. By **V5** there is
nothing to identify as an animal.

**2. What survives after identification is gone?** The relationships. A **low mass**, **stable**
(the centre of gravity sits well inside a wide base), **reaching forward and up** (the flow). What
remains is a configuration — *low, settled, forward-leaning* — with no creature attached to it.

**3. Is structure the same thing as identity?** On this evidence, no. The structure that survived
is **shared** — many resting animals, and even non-animal settling forms, would hold this same
low-stable-forward configuration. It carries the *kind of thing* (a low, settled, forward-reaching
mass), not the individual. The calf's identity needed the structure **plus** the specific
deviations that were stripped here — the hump, the proportions, the head. So structure is the
**frame identity hangs on**: necessary, and not the same as identity.

One honest qualification the experiment forced: structure is not perfectly identity-blank either.
A measure of "cow-ness" rode in the **proportions** of the arc through V1–V2 — the length, the
height of the topline. So structure carries the *category* while the specific deviations carry the
*individual*. Identity = a shared structure + the small deviations that ride on it. (Stated as what
these five versions showed; I'm not extending it past the experiment.)

Across A30–A35: what survives the article, observation, analysis, reduction — and now the floor.
Structure is the substrate; identity is the increment laid on top of it.
