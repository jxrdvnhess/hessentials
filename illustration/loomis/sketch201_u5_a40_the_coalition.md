## The construction log

**S0 — back-arc (given).** Pure organizing structure: a topline that *implies* a reclining body.

**S1 — + belly (chosen first).**
- *New job:* embodiment — the arc closes into a **volume**; a line becomes a body.
- *Strengthened:* the arc's organizing job is fulfilled — it now bounds a real mass instead of merely suggesting one.
- *Why first:* A39 showed recognition has a prerequisite — the figure must read as a *body* before it can be an animal or a breed. Nothing else can do its job until a body exists, and A38 found the belly the strongest single helper of the structure. It is the enabling move.

**S2 — + head & ear (chosen second).**
- *New job:* recognition — the body becomes an **animal**, oriented, head up; "a creature lying down" sharpens toward "an animal, probably a cow."
- *Strengthened:* embodiment (the volume gains a front and a neck-terminus) and the arc's head-to-hip gesture (now it ends in an actual head).
- *Why second:* with a body present, the head is the addition that most increases total usefulness — and it can only work *now*, because (A39) the head needs a body to belong to.

**S3 — + foreleg (chosen third).**
- *New job:* pose — the forward-folded leg states **resting / lying down** explicitly, not just "a body."
- *Strengthened:* embodiment (a weight-bearing limb) and the reclining reading.
- *Why third:* body and animal established, the next-largest gain is making the *pose* legible; the foreleg now has a chest to emerge from.

**S4 — + ground (chosen fourth).**
- *New job:* placement — the animal rests **on a surface**; weight and balance are anchored.
- *Strengthened:* the pose and the "resting" reading.
- *Why fourth:* a real gain, but it relates the animal to its environment rather than building the animal itself — secondary to making the animal and its pose first.

**S5 — + hump (chosen last).**
- *New job:* identity — now that a recognizable resting animal exists, the hump finally **fires** and specifies "a zebu / this calf."
- *Strengthened:* recognition becomes specific (genus → individual/breed).
- *Why last:* per A36 and A39 the hump (a naming mark) can only do its job once there is a recognizable animal to specify; placed earlier it was a meaningless bump. It is the correct final, individuating touch.

## What the coalition is

The order is not a preference ranking — it is a **dependency chain**: body (belly) → animal (head) →
pose (foreleg) → placement (ground) → identity (hump). Each addition was chosen because the previous
ones *unlocked* it. The three job-types from A37–A39 cooperate by respecting that order:
**organizing/completing must precede recognition, which must precede naming.** You cannot give a body
a breed before you give it a body. So maximizing total usefulness step-by-step *produces* the build
order on its own — which is the synthesis the unit was driving at: the jobs are not interchangeable,
and they are not free-order; identity assembles bottom-up.
