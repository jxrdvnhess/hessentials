## Ranking — least strange to most strange

1. shorter stems · 2. thick stems · 3. branches close · 4. one taut, one slack ·
5. branches uneven · 6. stems to the sides

## Writeup (≤150 words)

**Observations.** Three panels go nearly normal while keeping both stems: *shorter stems* reads as
fruit at a branch-fork; *thick stems* reads as one forked branch; *branches close* reads as one tree
with a doubled stem. *Taut/slack* resolves — the taut line holds, the slack one turns incidental.
*Branches uneven* stays odd but offers a partial story: hangs from the high branch, rests on the low
one. *Stems to the sides* is strangest — an even, deliberate cradle; botany becomes gesture.

**Explanations (separate).** Every de-stranged panel either merged the two sources into one or
ranked them. The strange panels keep two equal, separate sources. Candidate: strangeness tracks the
number of separate claimants the eye cannot reduce — not the number of stems. Of the offered
explanations, *two claims conflict* survives best, refined: two **equal and separate** claims.
*(≈140 words)*
