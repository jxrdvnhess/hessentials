## The argument that forced the merge (the room, briefly)

II, III, IV, and V were put in one room and asked what they each deny. All four deny the same thing:
that a mark has intrinsic value. *Jobs* are relational roles; *coalition value* is relational
standing; the *two currencies* are two kinds of relational payoff (meetings between marks buy unity;
relations between proportions buy identity); the *thin mark* is the same discovery at minimal scale.
Four scales of one discovery — between marks, between marks and the figure, between the thing and
the world, plus its pricing. They merged.

Two resisted. **Observation beats schema** is about where truth enters the drawing — the world, not
the rig — a claim about input, not about how pictures mean. **Natural vs intentional** is about who
the viewer believes made the relation — a claim about authorship, not content. Different questions;
they stayed.

## The three

1. Observation beats schema.
2. Meaning lives in relationships, not parts.
3. Relations read natural or intentional.

The three opens remain open, unchanged.
