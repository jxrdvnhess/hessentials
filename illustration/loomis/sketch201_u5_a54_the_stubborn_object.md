## Writeup (≤150 words)

**Observations.** The cup converted zero of six. The oval converted six of six on the same
relationships. The nest is the cleanest pair: it *named* the oval (egg) and could not name the cup —
it produced comedy instead. The conflicts resolved three ways on this page: absorbed as normal
(table, plinth, floating litter); explained by importing an agent (someone hung it, someone set it
there); or, where the relationship insists on the impossible (the *grown* curved stem), the picture
goes deliberate — surreal, a statement, addressed to the viewer.

**Explanations (separate, candidates).** Relationships propose; a closed object can veto. A53's
boundary sharpens: classification follows the relationship only as far as the object leaves the
question open. And when neither yields, the surplus doesn't vanish — it becomes story (agency) or
address (the strangeness register again). One reader, who built the page. *(~150 words)*
