## Writeup (≤150 words)

**Observations.** The ground reads fully natural. *Alone* reads as no situation — intention cannot be
asked without a second thing. The cradle flickers (bowl? hollow?). The string is the first mark that
could not have happened by itself — for a stone. The plinth makes an exhibit; the cage makes a
prisoner. Held ≠ presented: the cradle keeps the stone; the plinth shows it — aimed at the viewer.
Suspension is not automatically intentional: A49's apple hung naturally; the stone cannot — it
depends on whether the world supplies a hanger. Support became presentation without touching the
stone: raise the surface, narrow it, give it nothing else to do. Two category changes: *presented*
adds an audience; *constrained* adds an opponent.

**Explanation (separate, candidate).** The natural panels' supports exist for their own reasons
(ground). The intentional ones exist only for the stone (string, plinth, cage). A51's sentence
survives transfer. *(~148 words)*
