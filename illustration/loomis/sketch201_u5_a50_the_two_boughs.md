# Sketch 201 · Unit 5 · A50 — The Two Boughs

Tests the pointed-at lead from A49: is the thin mark's power *belonging* — membership in a larger
whole — or any contact? One apple, fixed dead center in every panel. Two boughs above it, left and
right. Only the thin mark changes. Page: `loomis/sketch201_u5_a50_the_two_boughs.png` (generator
`_a50_the_two_boughs.py`). Run before trusted.

## The five panels

**Bare.** The apple floats between the two bough tips. Unclaimed — odd, or falling.

**Stem to the left bough.** The apple is the left tree's. Instantly, completely, and with no other
change. A fully normal picture.

**Stem to the right bough.** The same untouched apple is now the right tree's. Ownership flipped
because the far end of one thin line landed somewhere else.

**Both stems.** Not doubly held. It reads as strange — an error, or a contrivance, or a fable about
two trees sharing one fruit. Two homes read as no ordinary home.

**Grazed by a line.** An equally thin line touches the apple and ends nowhere. The apple stays
unclaimed; the line reads as a wire, a scratch, a stray. Contact happened. Ownership didn't.

## What the page shows

Touch alone is not the mechanism: the grazing line touches exactly as the stem does and changes
almost nothing. What did the work, on this page, was where the mark's *other end* landed. The stem
panels flip the apple's owner with nothing else changed; the destination-less line has no such power.

Two smaller observations. Double membership did not add security — it subtracted normalcy; on this
page, twice-claimed reads as strangely as unclaimed. And the two single-stem panels are the
quietest pictures on the page: once the apple belongs, the panel stops asking questions. The bare
and both-stems panels are the ones that hold the eye. Claimed things rest; unclaimed and
over-claimed things ask.

The lead survives its test page: the mark's work here was telling the apple where it belongs, and a
mark with nowhere to point couldn't do it. The boat finding also re-confirms — nothing was broken in
any stem panel; this was never repair.

*Caveats owned:* one subject, one page; and the grazing line differs from a stem in more than
destination — orientation, length, and that it passes through rather than ends. A stricter version
would graze with a stem-shaped mark. Held as a supported lead, not a law.
