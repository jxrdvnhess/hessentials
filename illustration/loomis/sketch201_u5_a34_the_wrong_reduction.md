# Sketch 201 · Unit 5 · A34 — The Wrong Reduction

The same zebu calf, the same twelve marks as A33 — removed in the *wrong* order. The carrying
**structure** (back-arc, foreleg, belly-on-ground, rump, neck) is destroyed first; the small
**details** (ear, dewlap, muzzle, far ear, hind hoof) are protected to the end. Ten stages,
12 → 3 marks. Image: `loomis/sketch201_u5_a34_wrong_reduction.png` (generator
`_a34_wrong_reduction.py`). Compared by information, not by quality.

## What happens

The first removal — the back-arc — does it. At 11 marks the topline and body are gone and what
remains is a head with parts dangling off it and no animal beneath. From there the belly, rump,
and foreleg go and the figure becomes a floating head-cluster; by the final stages only an ear,
a dewlap-curve, and a muzzle-dot remain, sitting in space.

## Information, stage by stage

- **What becomes impossible to know.** Once the back-arc and the belly/foreleg are gone (by ~9
marks), the marks no longer report that this is a **cow**, that it is **resting**, its **pose**,
its **orientation**, its **size**, or even that it is an **animal**. The final three marks
(ear + dewlap + muzzle) carry none of that.
- **What becomes difficult to know.** Even the head loses its footing: with the neck and back
removed, the ears and nose float without a skull-to-body connection — readable maybe as "a small
head," not as which animal, and not as lying down.
- **What becomes misleading.** This is the sharp result. The protected details do not just fail —
they **misdirect**. The dewlap, cut free of the neck, reads as a "V" (a beak?); the two ears plus
the nose-dot cluster reads more like a small bird or an abstract face than a cow. Detail without
structure is not neutral leftover; it asserts the wrong thing.

## What this tells us about A33's leverage

Set the two sheets side by side at equal mark-counts. A33 at 8 marks is clearly the resting calf;
A34 at 8 marks is already a broken head-cluster. A33 at 3 marks still reads as the calf; A34 at 3
marks reads as nothing of the kind. **Same number of marks, opposite legibility** — the only
difference is which marks were kept. Removing the single back-arc (the first wrong step) cost more
information than removing six details did in all of A33.

So the leverage A33 found is real, and it is **asymmetric**: the relational structure carried
nearly all the information; the details rode on it and carried little alone. Kept in isolation,
the details did worse than vanish — they lied. (Stated from what these two sheets show, at matched
counts; not pushed past that. It rhymes with A28: detail and cleverness without the carrying
structure mislead.)
