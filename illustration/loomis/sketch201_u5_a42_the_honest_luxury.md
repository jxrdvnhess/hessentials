# Sketch 201 · Unit 5 · A42 — The Honest Luxury

Start from the completed coalition calf — which already works — and add five marks that improve it
but aren't required. For each: what improves, what new debt appears, is it worth the debt. Image:
`loomis/sketch201_u5_a42_honest_luxury.png` (generator `_a42_honest_luxury.py`). Run, not argued.

## The five luxuries

**Eye** (a small knockout on the head)
- *Improves:* the head gains a point of life and focus — the whole figure feels awake.
- *Debt:* small — it commits the head's facing and gaze; it must sit correctly.
- *Worth?* Yes, clearly. The largest gain for the smallest cost in the set — the bargain luxury.

**Dewlap** (a hanging curve under the throat)
- *Improves:* a little zebu/calf character at the throat.
- *Debt:* moderate — it must attach to the neck and chest; in this render it reads slightly detached,
so its attachment-debt is visibly unpaid. It is also partly redundant with the hump, which already
named the breed.
- *Worth?* Marginal. Small character gain against a real attachment debt.

**Tail** (a curve and tuft at the rear)
- *Improves:* completes the rear — a whole animal.
- *Debt:* modest — must originate at the tailhead; commits the rear.
- *Worth?* Optional and pleasant. The drawing read without it; this is charm, not need.

**Second ear**
- *Improves:* a touch of head symmetry / 3D.
- *Debt:* small — must sit correctly relative to the head turn.
- *Worth?* Barely. Near-pure decoration; the head read with one ear.

**Shoulder line** (an interior contour on the body)
- *Improves:* in principle, interior plane / volume.
- *Debt:* high — an interior line must be anatomically exact or it reads as a crease, a wound, or an
extra leg. In this render it reads ambiguously.
- *Worth?* No — here the debt meets or exceeds the gain. This is where luxury tips into liability.

## What the run found

The necessity threshold was already crossed before A42 began: the **hump** was the last *necessary*
mark; the **eye** is the first *optional* one. So "when does enrichment stop being necessary and start
being optional?" — at the end of the coalition. The drawing worked; everything here is a luxury.

But the more useful threshold is *inside* the optional zone. The luxuries are not equal: they descend
from **nearly-free improvement** (the eye) to **expensive and possibly harmful** (the shoulder line).
The **honest luxury** is the one whose improvement clearly exceeds its debt — the eye. The dishonest
one is the mark whose debt meets or beats its gain — the shoulder line, which buys ambiguity. Between
them sit the dewlap, tail, and second ear: real but small gains against small-to-moderate debts —
take them or leave them.

This is where restraint becomes an accounting decision, not a taste one: keep adding while gain
clearly exceeds debt; stop when the next mark's debt catches up to its gain. On this calf that line
falls after the eye and before the shoulder line. (Said of this build and these five — not a fixed
ranking of features; the recurring tax left unpaid.)
