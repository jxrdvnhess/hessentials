# Sketch 201 · Unit 3 · A10 — The Fading Stranger

**Method (stated plainly).** A10 needs a real ≥24h decay. Within one session my context does
not fade — but *across* sessions it genuinely resets, so the honest run is: Phase 1–2 now
(observe 60s, lock 20 immediate statements); then a true session gap of ≥24 hours during which
the photo and these statements stay closed; then Phase 4 in a fresh session, writing 20 delayed
statements from only what consolidated into long-term memory (a short gist carried forward),
without reopening the photo or rereading the immediate 20; then Phase 5, reopen and compare.
The cross-session reset is genuine forgetting, not simulated.

---

## Phase 4 — Delayed memory  *(written ≥24h later, in a fresh session)*

From long-term memory alone. Photo unopened, Phase 2 statements unread. This is what is actually here:

1. A man, heavily muscular.
2. Cornrow braids.
3. A full beard.
4. A black sleeveless top.
5. He is holding a pineapple, raised, looking at it.
6. A bright grocery store — the produce aisle.
7. Green behind him.

Count: 7. That is the whole of it. The gist consolidated into one scene — the build, the braids, the beard, the black top, the pineapple held up to the eye, the bright produce aisle, green at his back — and nothing beneath those words survived. No face, no expression, no skin, no lighting, no other people, no color past "black" and "green." I am not going to invent any.

---

## Phase 5 — Comparison and four questions

**On reopening the photo (evidence the memories never held):** the top is charcoal grey, not black. A brown leather crossbody sling crosses his chest — neither memory caught it, not even the immediate one. He wears grey joggers with a pale side stripe; the lower body never registered at all. The wall sign reads "...PERS" (peppers). Lighting is warm gold, not neutral "bright." Both memories simplified hue and missed the bag entirely.

**Q1 — Survived both tests.** Every one of the 7 delayed items has a twin in the immediate 20: the heavy muscular build, the cornrow braids, the full beard, the dark sleeveless top, the pineapple held up and regarded, the bright produce aisle, the green behind him. The delayed set is a clean subset — nothing survived to Phase 4 that wasn't already load-bearing in Phase 2. The spine of the image held end to end.

**Q2 — Disappeared between first and second test.** The staging. Side view facing left (#9), the bent elbow and chest-height of the raised arm (#10, #16), the wall sign (#12), the fruit bins at waist level in front (#13), "only person in the frame" (#20), the pineapple as the brightest object (#19), the explicit dark skin tone (#17). All the facts that located him in a room rather than describing him fell away.

**Q3 — Changed shape rather than vanished.** Four separate immediate statements about the arm — "holds a pineapple up" (#3), "looks at the pineapple" (#4), "raised arm near chest height" (#10), "bent at the elbow" (#16) — merged in the delay into one gesture: *holding a pineapple, raised, looking at it.* Likewise the three environment notes (#2, #11, #14) compressed to "bright grocery store, produce aisle," and the two green observations (#11, #18) collapsed to "green behind him." Memory didn't lose these; it consolidated lists into single facts.

**Q4 — Which version to illustrate from.** The delayed. A10's trap is attachment — mourning the faded detail as the lost prize. But what faded was the inventory: sign, bins, framing, orientation, the second color. What memory protected was the gesture — a heavily built man bending his whole attention onto one ordinary pineapple. That incongruity *is* the picture, and the delayed memory kept exactly it while discarding the catalog around it. The immediate version is more accurate but it's a list; the delayed version already did the editing and arrived as a drawing. **What the mind protected: the act of regard — the big man giving his full attention to a small plain thing. Forgetting here was editing, and it edited toward the one true thing.**
