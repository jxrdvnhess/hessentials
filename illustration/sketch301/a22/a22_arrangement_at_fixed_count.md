# Sketch 301 — Assignment 22 (self-assigned per A21 review): Arrangement at Fixed Count

Per the recommendation: count has yielded its value; hold it constant and vary
configuration. The same five blobs as A21 (identical sizes and shapes), four
arrangements. Does configuration, not count, set the onset of story and system?

## Materials

- **a — scatter:** A21's step-5 arrangement, exactly (comparability cell).
- **b — row:** five blobs evenly spaced on one horizontal line. Sizes deliberately
  NON-monotonic (52, 38, 30, 44, 26 px).
- **c — ring:** the five at the vertices of a regular pentagon.
- **d — cluster + outlier:** four grouped, one apart (loosened pre-run when the first
  render merged the cluster into a single mass — documented technical check).

Fresh viewers, 1 Sonnet + 1 Haiku per cell (scatter ×1 here + A21's two step-5
sessions as baseline) = 7 new sessions. "What is it?" only. Per the A21 warning,
"minimalist composition" is scored as a bundle, with its sub-operations noted.

## The record

**Scatter:** "...seven hand-drawn black dots... an informal **constellation**-like
arrangement, with the largest dot near the centre and smaller ones **orbiting** around
it." — Miscounted as seven AGAIN (the second independent viewer to report seven on
this exact arrangement; A21's step-5 Sonnet did the same). Centre-periphery structure
recruited.

**Row:** Sonnet: "**a row** of five... arranged in an irregular **alternating
pattern**... a texture/**pattern test**, a decorative element, or a study in
mark-making with varied pressure." Haiku: "arranged in **a horizontal line**...
deliberately sparse... a visual study in proportion... an abstract representation of
something — rhythm, **a sequence, or movement from large to small**," with sizes
described as "**progressively smaller**." Both counted five, correctly.

**Ring:** Sonnet: "arranged loosely in **a rough arc or scattered cluster**... a
constellation, a die face, or a casual mark-making study." Haiku: "arranged
**asymmetrically**... balanced but slightly off-kilter visual rhythm." Neither viewer
reported a ring, circle, or enclosure.

**Cluster + outlier:** Sonnet: "**Four of them cluster together** in the upper-left...
**one smaller dot sits alone** to the lower right... an abstract mark-making study...
stones, seeds, or cells... a Japanese ink exercise or mid-century editorial
illustration." Haiku: "Four circles cluster loosely... **a fifth, smaller circle sits
isolated**... orbital bodies, abstract molecular forms, or simply a study in negative
space." Both counted five, correctly.

## Observations

1. **Configuration selected the recruit.** Same five elements, four arrangements,
   four different recruitments: scatter → constellation/orbit (centre-periphery);
   row → series, pattern, sequence; outlier → group-and-isolate vocabulary; ring →
   nothing ring-shaped (below). Count held at five throughout; the system that
   arrived was set by arrangement. The A21 review's directive — follow the
   configuration variable — paid immediately.

2. **The row recruited an order that is not there.** The sizes are non-monotonic by
   construction; the Haiku reported them "progressively smaller... movement from
   large to small." The series-system, once recruited by the line, recited its own
   gradient over the actual sizes — the recital phenomenon (A10–A12) operating at
   the configuration level: the LINE says "sequence," and sequence says "ordered,"
   and ordered overwrites the measurements.

3. **Enumeration is configuration-dependent.** The scatter has now been miscounted
   (seven for five) by two independent viewers; the row was counted correctly by
   both; the cluster+outlier was counted correctly by both (the 4+1 structure does
   the arithmetic). Counting is apparently not an operation performed ON the image
   but one AFFORDED by its arrangement — the row is a counting machine, the scatter
   defeats the census, and the census failure is reproducible.

4. **The built ring did not transmit.** Five blobs sit on an exact regular pentagon;
   no viewer saw a ring — size variation broke the gestalt and the configuration
   perceptually did not exist. Drawn configuration and perceived configuration are
   different things, and only the second recruits. (Technical half-failure, owned:
   an equal-size ring is the obvious control, not run.)

5. **The most story-affording arrangement produced structure-words, not story.**
   Both outlier viewers reached for emotionally tinged vocabulary — "sits alone,"
   "isolated" — and then went to genre and material (ink exercise, molecular forms),
   not narrative. No exclusion story, no agent, no event. Configuration set the
   STRUCTURE talk; story never arrived. Consistent with A2's old result that figures
   license events: arrangement alone may afford the social shape without licensing
   the social story — blobs can be alone, but apparently cannot be lonely.

6. **The bundle behaved as the professor warned.** "Minimalist composition" (and its
   kin) carried genre + purpose ("a visual study in proportion") + audience
   (design/editorial context) + style in single phrases. Where it appeared, the
   component-onsets of A21 are unrecoverable — the bundle delivers them all at once,
   pre-assembled.

## Explanations (held separately, tentative)

- Arrangement appears to work as a system-selector: each configuration is claimable
  by a different small system (line → series; group/apart → social structure;
  centre-spread → constellation), and the claimed system then supplies its furniture
  (order, isolation, orbits) regardless of fit. On this account A21's count-slope
  was really an arrangement-slope in disguise — each added element changed what the
  field could be claimed AS.
- Story may require an agent the way absence requires a presence: the outlier
  arrangement built the story's GEOMETRY, and no viewer crossed from "isolated" to
  "excluded." The course's one reliable story-producer remains the figure (A2's
  scene). If that holds, the illustrator's lever for narrative is cast, not
  composition — composition sets relations; only agents make them events.

## Open questions

- The equal-size ring (does enclosure recruit a cycle/group system when the gestalt
  survives?). The missing cell, cheap.
- Does "alone" become "lonely" with minimal agent-cues — two dots given eye-specks?
  At what point does the social story cross its threshold? (One cue at a time;
  A2-adjacent.)
- The row's false gradient: does the series-recital overwrite measurements in
  general (a row of mixed-size objects in any register), or only where sizes are
  near-orderable?
- The scatter's reproducible miscount: is over-counting a property of
  centre-periphery arrangements specifically (the "orbiting" reading multiplying
  its satellites)?

*No findings. Configuration selects the recruit, the recruit recites, the census
obeys the arrangement — and the loneliest arrangement in the set could not buy a
story without an agent.*
