# Sketch 301 — Assignment 21: Recruitment Threshold

As assigned: when does recruitment begin? Pure accumulation — one form family, five
steps, each step adding one element, earlier elements never moving. No text, no
labels, no known symbols or diagrams. Components of recruitment (category, purpose,
story, system, audience, attribution) measured separately. A slope is a valid result.

## Materials

Irregular soft-edged ink-dark blob forms on the warm ground, 1 → 5, fixed placements
(one placement adjusted before any session to break an accidental face-triad at step
3 — technical check, documented). One fresh viewer per step per ladder; **two
independent ladders** for replication: Ladder I all Sonnet, Ladder II all Haiku — one
viewer per step, no history, 10 sessions.

## Observations

1. **Recruitment is not one event — its components arrived separately, in an order.**
   Ladder I: material-category at one element; hedged object-candidates at two; the
   first event-verb ("dropped" — something HAPPENED to these marks) at four; system,
   purpose, and attributed intent together at five. No purpose-talk, no story, no
   system for the first three steps. The brief's instruction to measure components
   separately was the right instruction: they have different onsets.

2. **No sharp threshold — a slope (Ladder I).** Each added element bought roughly one
   additional component. The assignment's stated alternative result is the result.

3. **Ladder II bypassed the ladder entirely.** The Haiku cohort recruited a GENRE at
   ONE element — "a minimalist composition," complete with attributed intent
   ("refusing to announce itself") — and never left it. Steps 1 through 5 produced
   near-identical responses; accumulation added nothing because the genre-system
   absorbed every new element as more composition. For this cohort the threshold sits
   at or below a single blob. **The threshold is a property of the viewer-stimulus
   pair, not of the stimulus** — the cohort difference dwarfed the element-count
   difference.

4. **Recruitment, once made, flattens accumulation.** Ladder II is A3 and A12 in
   miniature: an installed frame (there a card, a recital; here a genre) renders
   subsequent evidence inert. The ladder measured a slope in one cohort and the
   slope's preemption in the other — both informative.

5. **The maker arrived before the story.** In both ladders, attribution
   (hand-drawn, brushed, stamped; "intentional") preceded purpose and narrative —
   in Ladder II it was present at step one. Confound owned: my ground and wobbly
   edges carry hand-cues, so madeness was invited by the rendering itself. But the
   ORDER (maker before story before system, in the cohort that had an order) is data
   the confound doesn't erase.

6. **The hedge zone sat in the middle.** Ladder I hedged at steps 2–3 (the
   candidate zone — "almost like," "reads loosely like," and one near-abstention:
   "just three marks on a field"), and resolved by step 5, where the system absorbed
   everything. Ladder II never hedged — the genre never strained. Hedge-density
   again tracked system-grip, not stimulus complexity (consistent with A19–A20's
   instrument mapping).

7. **Two small replications in passing:** viewer-relativity stated BY a viewer for
   the first time ("depending on how you look at them"); and counting failed exactly
   where grouping began (step 5 reported as seven marks — the cluster was read,
   not enumerated; the recital of a scatter needs no census).

## Explanations (held separately, tentative)

- The component order in Ladder I (material → object-candidates → event → 
  system/intent) reads like recruitment assembling bottom-up when nothing richer is
  available, one relation at a time: two elements afford a pair, four afford an
  event, five afford a practice. Ladder II shows the same stack arriving pre-built
  when a genre will take the whole image at once. The "threshold," then, may be the
  point where SOME available system can claim the WHOLE field — and that point
  depends on the viewer's inventory, not on element count per se.
- Ladder II's genre ("minimalist composition") is the icon-lexicon of A20-D
  operating at image scale: a shared absorber that converts any sparse field into a
  member of a known class. Sparse warm-ground images may be unusually vulnerable to
  this absorber — a brand-relevant fact, since the brand makes exactly such images.

## Open questions

- Does the Ladder I component-order replicate on a neutral ground (no hand-cues —
  hard-edged vector forms on white)? The maker-first result needs that control.
- Is there a cohort or context in which the genre-absorber fails and a Haiku-type
  viewer walks the slope? (Conversely: what besides blobs makes a Sonnet-type viewer
  skip it?)
- The brief excluded relations by design (accumulation only). The obvious sequel
  variable is ARRANGEMENT at fixed count: five blobs scattered vs five in a row vs
  five in a ring — does configuration, not count, set the onset of story and system?
  (A50's lattice and A21's scatter suggest yes; not run.)
- "Just three marks on a field" — the second near-abstention in two assignments,
  again mid-ladder, again adjacent to hedges. Is the candidate zone (where nothing
  has won yet) the only place description gets to speak?

*No findings. A slope in one cohort, a bypass in the other, and a threshold that
moved into the viewer.*
