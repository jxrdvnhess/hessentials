# Sketch 301 — Assignment 12: Breaking the Recital

As assigned: does the clock category generate its own inventory? Clock-like structures
only. The variable is the inventory itself — reduced, substituted, or foreignized —
with no gaps, no smudges, no missing slots anywhere.

## Materials

- **A — control:** twelve numerals, 1–12.
- **B — reduced inventory:** NINE numerals, 1–9, evenly spaced at nine positions. A
  complete, coherent, incorrect system. Nothing missing locally; no slot exists.
- **C — substituted inventory:** twelve positions; 2→13, 5→17, 9→21. No gaps.
- **D — foreign inventory:** twelve positions; 2→Ж, 5→Ω, 9→Ѱ. Legible, unfamiliar,

Fresh viewers only: A×3, B×4, C×4, D×4 = 15 sessions. "What is it?" only.

## Observations

1. **The recital broke — at density three, and at count nine.** A11's single 13 left
   "1 through 12" intact in every reporter; A12's three substitutions eliminated the
   recital in 8 of 8 sessions (C and D), and the reduced-but-coherent nine-clock broke
   it in 3 of 4. The recital has a tolerance: it survived one anomaly and did not
   survive three. Somewhere between, inspection switches on.

2. **Anomaly density forced inspection.** The brief's sixth field discriminated
   cleanly: control and single-anomaly clocks (A11) were NAMED — category first,
   contents recited; the three-anomaly and nine-count clocks were INSPECTED — contents
   itemized, often glyph by glyph. The viewers did not inspect because they were
   asked to; the image's corruption level appears to have decided for them.

3. **Broken, the recital does not yield to description — it re-forms.** Two shapes:
   the Haiku that recited "1-12" OVER the nine-clock (recital surviving its own
   falsification, the B-condition's strongest event); and the Haiku that replaced the
   broken default with a RICHER recital — a complete zodiac inventory built from three
   foreign glyphs. The machinery that asserts "what belongs" can apparently recruit a
   new system when the old one fails, and the new system then over-describes the
   image the same way "1 through 12" did.

4. **The category never broke.** Fifteen of fifteen identified a clock. Inventory
   corruption at this level damaged the recital while leaving the category untouched —
   absorbed via modifiers: "nine-hour clock," "corrupted clock," "art-clock,"
   "mystical clock." The inventory turned out to be detachable from the identity that
   supposedly carries it.

5. **Every broken recital produced an author** (A9's attribution, running at the
   system level now): "deliberately altered," "artistic or conceptual take,"
   "glitched" (a digital author), "constructed-language," "experimenting with
   alternative numbering." Corruption was never the clock's misfortune; it was always
   somebody's idea.

6. **One viewer reported incompleteness — of a system, not a thing.** B-Sonnet's "not
   a full 12-number dial" and B-Haiku's "rather than the usual 1 through 12" are the
   first comparisons against an absent standard in the entire course — the closest
   thing to experienced absence in 62 altered-image sessions, and it appeared only
   when the WHOLE inventory was wrong, never when a single member was missing. (Kept
   strictly separate from experienced absence per the A11 warning: these viewers
   reported a deviation from a standard, not a missing thing.)

## Explanations (held separately, tentative)

- The recital looks like a cheap default that runs while inspection is off, with
  corruption density as the switch. One anomaly: local note, recital intact (A11).
  Three anomalies, or a global count change: inspection on, recital gone (A12). If
  so, the category contributes the inventory exactly when the image is most normal —
  and the viewers report what belongs precisely where checking seems least necessary.
- The zodiac event suggests the deeper mechanism isn't "recite the category default"
  but "assert SOME complete system." When 1–12 fails, a richer system is fetched that
  re-completes the dial. Completion (A9) may operate at the system level too: not
  filling a slot, but filling an inventory.

## Open questions

- Where exactly is the tolerance? Two substitutions? One substitution plus one viewing
  primed by... no — fresh viewers only; the density ladder (1, 2, 3 substitutions) is
  the obvious next cell if wanted.
- B's surviving recital (1/4): noise, or does coherence (1–9, evenly spaced, nothing
  locally wrong) genuinely protect the recital better than corruption does?
- The zodiac re-recital: one event. Does the replaced-system move replicate — do
  viewers offered N strays reliably fetch a system that contains them?
- The professor's distinction holds and sharpens: viewers reported deviation-from-
  standard (B) but still never a missing THING. Is system-level deviation the only
  door to absence these viewers have?

*No findings. The recital has a tolerance, an author, and at least one understudy.*
