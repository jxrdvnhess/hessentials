# Sketch 301 — Assignment 13: System Replacement

As assigned: was A12's zodiac event noise or phenomenon? One domain only — playing
cards. Each corrupted image permits the expected system AND at least one plausible
alternative.

## Materials

- **A — control:** the seven of clubs, standard layout.
- **B — impossible member:** the **15 of clubs** — standard vocabulary, impossible
  rank, fifteen pips. Permits: corrupted/novelty standard card; extended deck.

### B — the 15 of clubs

All four stayed inside the standard system and reported the impossibility from within
it: "the 15 of Clubs... **a rank of 15, which does not exist in a standard 52-card
deck**... digitally generated or novelty card" / "a playing card that doesn't exist in
a standard deck... possibly from an extended deck for a special game" / "fictional or
custom... games that use extended decks." Anomaly named, author assigned (novelty,
custom, digital), system retained.

### C — the seven of crescents

All four kept the system's GRAMMAR and swapped the vocabulary: "the **Seven of a
suit**, but... crescent moons as its suit symbol" / "**a '7 of moons'**... could be
from a custom or novelty deck, a tarot-adjacent deck, or simply a design piece" /
"from a themed or custom deck — possibly a **lunar/celestial themed set**." The
linguistic form is the result: "seven of moons" — the standard system bent to contain
the foreign member, and the named alternatives stayed thin (a themed deck is the
standard system wearing a costume).

### D — the arcana-shaped card

All four left the playing-card system entirely, instantly: "It is a **tarot card**...
numbered XXI... **consistent with traditional tarot corner indexing**" / "a tarot-style
card... numbered XXI **like the Major Arcana's World card**" / "a modern, minimalist
**tarot card** design... suggests The World card... **though the sun imagery is
unconventional for it**" / "the **Sun card (XXI — Roman numeral 21, which is the Sun's
position in the Major Arcana)**."

That last response deserves its own line: XXI is the World; the Sun is XIX. The viewer
replaced the system AND ran the replacement's recital wrongly — a confident false
member-fact, asserted with the same fluency as "numerals 1 through 12." Another viewer
ran the same recital correctly and managed the emblem-tension openly ("unconventional
for it"). My invented banner was validated as tarot convention by one viewer
("consistent with traditional tarot corner indexing").

## Observations

1. **Replacement is real.** The A12 zodiac event replicated under design: 4 of 4
   viewers of the arcana-shaped card recruited a complete alternative system, named
   its members, and read the image through it. Not noise — phenomenon, at least for
   corruptions of this shape.

2. **Replacement tracked the LOCATION of the corruption, not its amount** (one
   corruption per card throughout): impossible member VALUE (15) → system retained,
   anomaly authored; foreign member IDENTITY (crescent) → system stretched, grammar
   kept, vocabulary extended ("seven of moons"); foreign GRAMMAR (Roman numerals,
   emblem, banner) → system replaced outright. The deeper the corruption sat in the
   system's structure, the richer the recruited replacement.

3. **The replacement arrives with its own recital.** "XXI is the Sun's position" is a
   false fact about the REPLACEMENT system, recited with full confidence — the same
   behavior "1 through 12" showed over the nine-clock, now running on a freshly
   recruited system. The recital phenomenon is not clock-specific and not
   original-system-specific; whatever system is in power recites.

4. **Nobody merely described, and nobody was systemless.** 14 of 14 viewers recruited
   some system; zero fell back to raw description ("a white rectangle with marks").
   When the standard system couldn't hold the image, viewers did not hold LESS system —
   they held a different one. (A12 Obs 3 carried, now under design rather than
   accident.)

5. **The stretched system has a grammar of its own:** "the seven of [moons]" — rank
   retained, suit slot treated as an open variable. The standard system apparently
   exposes designated extension points (suit, theme, deck) where foreign members can
   be installed without replacement, while other points (indexing, layout, structure)
   trigger wholesale re-platforming when touched. (One domain; held lightly.)

6. **Anomaly-plus-author continues** (A9–A12 unbroken): novelty, custom, digitally
   generated, artistic, design-forward. Every deviation belonged to somebody's
   intention. No viewer has yet met a corrupted system that was nobody's idea.

## Explanations (held separately, tentative)

- The three outcomes order themselves as least-cost repairs: a wrong VALUE is cheapest
  to hold as an authored anomaly inside the standard system; a wrong MEMBER costs
  more, and the system pays by opening a variable slot; wrong GRAMMAR can't be repaired
  locally at all, and the cheapest available move becomes fetching a system whose
  grammar already matches (tarot owns Roman numerals + emblem + banner). Replacement
  as repair-of-last-resort, priced by depth. One domain of evidence; not promoted.
- The replacement recital (Obs 3) suggests the recital is a property of system-holding
  as such, not of familiarity: any system in power generates members and facts on
  credit. If so, an illustrator who triggers re-platforming inherits the NEW system's
  recital — including its errors — with no further input. The image stops supplying
  content the moment the system takes over.

## Open questions

- Does the depth-gradient transfer to a second domain (calendars: wrong date /
  foreign month-name / zodiacal grid)? One domain so far.
- Is there a corruption that produces system ABANDONMENT — raw description, no
  recruitment? Fourteen of fourteen recruited; the course has never seen a systemless
  answer to a system-shaped image.
- The C-cells' thin alternatives ("lunar deck"): stable hybrid, or a waystation that a
  second look would resolve toward replacement? (Single viewings only here.)
- The replacement's recital errors (Sun/XXI): how load-bearing are false member-facts
  in viewers' readings of REAL symbolic images? This is the assignment's most
  brand-relevant edge — editorial imagery that touches a system's grammar inherits
  that system's folklore, true and false alike.
