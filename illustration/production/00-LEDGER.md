# 501-P — Production Ledger

**Course correction, 2026-06-11: from investigation to production.**
Volume over theory. Generate, review, select, reject. Most entries
should lose — the objective is judgment, and the fastest path to
judgment is comparison.

Standing principle (faculty): **anatomy and observation are not
separate disciplines.** Anatomy is observation; construction
knowledge is what allows an observation to survive translation.

Standing question: **can Hessentials draw human beings as confidently
as it draws rooms?**

## Batch 1 — 2026-06-11 (batch1.py)

Two entrants, two deliberately new problems.

### P1 — The Reader by the Window — LOSER (instructive)

Seated + profile: two new pose families attempted at once, both
failed jointly. The head survived (profile contour with brow and
nose). The body collapsed into an ambiguous sack — the back curve and
chest line never enclose a torso, the figure hovers inside the
chair's lines rather than sitting on the seat plane, the book
vanished into the hand cluster, and the window's thrown-light edges
read as a falling sheet.

**The lesson, and it is the batch's real product: vocabulary does not
transfer across pose families.** Eight weeks of standing-man
vocabulary were worthless the moment he sat down. A new pose family
requires construction first — pelvis box on the seat plane, thigh
cylinders, THEN contour. I skipped construction because accumulated
vocabulary made me feel fluent. Fluency in one pose is not fluency.
This is Jordan's volumes note returning with fresh evidence, exactly
as the faculty predicted it would.

Protocol: seated returns in a future batch WITH the construction
scaffold. The failure stays in the corpus.

### P2 — The Woman Watering — WINNER (with corrections noted)

She reads immediately: a woman, back view, bun and neck, the dress
falling from the shoulders and breaking at the body's contact points,
weight settled, flats on the floor, the can tipped toward the plant.
First woman in the corpus; the dress was the first drape problem and
it mostly behaved.

Corrections owed before any page use: the raised arm has no elbow
(shoulder-to-can in one stiff line); the hand-handle junction is
muddy; the plant's leaves are scribble, not made marks (a known
banned ditch); the plant stand reads spindly.

### Batch verdict

1 winner, 1 loser. Correct ratio for a first batch. The corpus now
contains: a woman, a dress, a profile head, a failed seated figure,
and the transfer-failure lesson — which is worth more than a second
winner would have been.

## Batch 2 — 2026-06-11 (batch2.py)

One scene, two entrants — someone carrying groceries. Production
doctrine applied: began with the scene, drew first, judged second.
Prediction registered before rendering: asymmetry wins.

### P3 — Symmetric carry — LOSER (by design, and decisively)

A bag in each hand, even stance. Renders as a man-shaped coat rack:
mirror symmetry, no weight, no story, nothing happening anywhere in
the body. The cleanest controlled demonstration in the corpus of the
old correction "mechanical symmetry is the corpse" — now proven
against its own twin rather than asserted.

### P4 — Asymmetric carry — WINNER

One heavy bag pulling the right arm taut, shoulder dropped, spine
leaning away to counterweight; the second bag cradled against the
chest; mid-step, back heel just lifting. The body reads as physics —
the load redraws the whole figure, which is anatomy and observation
operating as one discipline. Corrections owed: the greens standing
out of the cradled bag failed to read (two strokes lost against the
bag's top edge — the observed detail needs clearance to be seen);
the cradled-bag/shoulder junction is slightly ambiguous.

### Batch finding

A carried weight is a drawing of the whole body, not of the arm
holding it. And the controlled pair beats the lone drawing as a
teaching instrument: P3 alone would read as merely dull; next to P4
it explains exactly WHY it is dull. Comparison is the fastest
judgment, as the protocol claims.

## Batch 3 — 2026-06-11 (batch3.py)

Two situations where the world acts on the body, per the faculty
note: the situations are teaching the anatomy.

### P5 — Folding Laundry (the sheet-fold moment) — WINNER

The woman's second appearance (same bun, same flats — inhabitants
accumulate). Arms wide at the sheet's corners; the sheet falls in two
swaying panels that frame her like wings; basket at her feet with
cloth over the rim. The symmetry is honest here because the task
demands it — you DO stand symmetric holding a sheet wide — which
sharpens the Batch 2 finding: symmetry is the corpse only when
nothing causes it. Caused symmetry is alive. Corrections owed: the
bun drifted toward the nape and reads ambiguous; the sheet's top
edge behind her shoulders is too sparse to bind the two falls into
one cloth.

### P6 — Reaching for a Book — WINNER AFTER REPAIR, with debts

First render failed mechanically: the hand missed the bookcase by a
hundred pixels and the tipped book floated between them. **A
consequence drawing fails exactly where cause and effect
disconnect — the reach must touch what it reaches for.** One repair
pass moved the case to meet the hand; the book now tips out against
his fingertip. The stretch reads: elongated torso, hem riding up on
the reaching side, heels off the floor, counterbalance arm out.
Debts: the forearm merges with the case's upright; the raised heels
are the weakest passage (toe-wedge feet, calves not working);
worth a construction look before this pose family recurs.

### Batch verdict

Two winners, one mechanical failure caught and repaired in-batch.
Corpus at six entries: 4 winners, 2 losers, every verdict reasoned.
The "caused symmetry" refinement and the "consequence must touch its
cause" rule are the batch's takings.

## Expression studies, tranche 1 of the twenty — 2026-06-11
## (expressions1.py — the first faces in the corpus)

Six faces, six emotions, no environments. Hand-placed, not rigged.

- **E2 concentration — WINNER.** Brows down-and-in with the knit
  mark, narrowed lids, iris marks beneath. Everything converges; it
  reads focused at a glance.
- **E3 exhaustion — WINNER, strongest of six.** Heavy half lids,
  under-eye weight, cheek lines, fallen mouth on a longer face. Reads
  as a specific worn person, not a tired-symbol.
- **E5 uncertainty — WINNER.** Inner brows raised, pupils carried
  aside, mouth pulled to one corner. The sideways pupils carry it —
  directed gaze is the single strongest emotion mark found today.
- **E4 amusement — marginal winner.** One brow up + asymmetric smile
  read warm; the crescent eyes drift toward closed-happy.
- **E1 relief — LOSER.** Reads as serene/asleep, not relieved. Relief
  is a moment-after emotion; the face alone may not carry it without
  the body's exhale.
- **E6 contentment — LOSER.** Nearly indistinguishable from E1.
  **The neighbor-emotion problem:** soft-symmetric states (relief,
  contentment, serenity) blur together at this economy; directed
  states (concentration, uncertainty) separate cleanly.

**Tranche lessons:** (1) directed features — pupils, knit, asymmetric
pulls — carry single emotions; undirected softness needs another
carrier. (2) All six faces float: no necks. Severed-head read; necks
and shoulder hints are mandatory from tranche 2. (3) A family
resemblance persists across all six (same nose stroke, same
construction) — half rig is still speaking. Score: 3 winners, 1
marginal, 2 losers. Running corpus: 12 entries.

## Emotional moments, tranche 1 — 2026-06-11 (moments1.py)

The two emotions that lost as isolated faces, retested with whole
bodies per the faculty correction. First front-view bodies in the
corpus.

### M1 — The Warm Mug (contentment) — WINNER

The woman, third appearance, first time facing us. Head bowed, eyes
closed, small smile, shoulders curled forward, arms folding in, both
hands on the mug, ankles crossed against the counter. **The
correction is confirmed: contentment failed as a face and reads as a
body.** The emotion lives in the curl of the shoulders and the wrap
of the hands; the face only agrees with it. Debts: the mug reads as
a small box (held objects need their object-logic — ellipse, handle —
or they parcel-ify); the hands/mug junction is mushy; she leans more
by implication than contact.

### M2 — Work Finished (relief, the painter) — LOSER, half-good

The slack half succeeds completely: the roller hanging from the
loose hand nearly to the floor reads instantly as the day being
over; head dropped, knee soft. The gesture half fails: the
hand-on-the-back-of-the-neck arm, drawn front-on, collapses into an
unreadable mass beside his head — the forearm is occluded and what
remains reads as a growth, not a gesture. **New finding: gestures
have legible viewpoints.** Hand-behind-neck is a profile gesture;
choosing the view is part of drawing the emotion. M2 returns in
three-quarter or profile.

### Tranche verdict

1 winner, 1 loser. The face-vs-body question is settled in the
body's favor for soft emotions. Running corpus: 14 entries, 8
winners. Remaining of the twenty: 4 expressions, 4 moments/ordinary.

Faculty hierarchy recorded first: face < posture < activity; the
strongest work appears when all three agree — faces are members of a
team. MASCOT WATCH standing: recurring inhabitants must accumulate
observation, not recognition ("I know this person," never "there's
the character again").

### H1 — Flowers, Before the Bell — WINNER, with debts

Profile at the door (viewpoint lesson applied — the hover needed a
profile to be seen). The raised finger stops short of the bell; the
emotion is the gap. Real profile face — brow, nose, lips, chin — a
first. Debts: the gap could be a touch wider to read at thumbnail;
the flower stems tangle with the hanging hand; the bell is easy to

### H2 — Sitting After Difficult Work — WINNER

**The seated figure, avenged.** Batch 1's reader collapsed because
construction was skipped; this one was built bones-first — pelvis on
the seat plane, thigh forward, shin dropped, foot flat — then
contoured. He sits convincingly: the long spent curve of the back,
head dropped, forearm on the thigh, hand hanging slack between the
knees. No props; the body says the whole sentence. Direct
confirmation of the toolbox amendment in production: same pose
family, failure → success, and the only variable changed was
construction first.

### Tranche verdict

2 winners. Running corpus: 16 entries, 10 winners. The seated pose
family is now open territory. Remaining of the twenty: 4 expressions
(necks mandatory), 2 moments/ordinary.

## Human moments, tranche 3 — 2026-06-11 (moments3.py)

Moments, not actions, per the faculty note. Symbol watch applied.

### H3 — Deciding Not to Knock — WINNER, with debts

The invited sequel: same door, same man, same flowers. The body has
turned away; the face hasn't finished turning; the feet answer the
question the head is still asking (pointing away, trailing heel up).
The bell untouched. The flowers changed meaning by changing posture —
from offering, held up, to baggage, hanging — which is the symbol
watch passed: the object earned its second meaning through the
situation. Debts: the flowers nearly vanished (a changed-meaning
object needs MORE presence at the moment its meaning changes, not
less); the turned head reads subtly and asks the reader to consult
the feet — possibly a virtue, logged as a question.

### H4 — Noticing the Room Is Empty — LOSER, instructive

The emptiness is drawn; the noticing is not. The composition works —
small figure boxed in the doorframe, the room as active negative
space, one chair, and a single nail where something hung (emptiness
with a history). But the realization never attached to the body: the
stopping hand on the jamb merged into the frame line and was lost,
and without it he reads as standing, not stopping. The two floor
lines also read as a step — flat elevation strains against deep
rooms. **Finding: realization is a verb and needs its own gesture
vocabulary (the checked step, the hand finding the jamb); emptiness
alone is a noun and cannot carry the moment.**

### Tranche verdict

1 winner, 1 instructive loser. Running corpus: 18 entries, 11
winners. The anticipation finding now has its negative twin: the gap
can close both ways, and both directions are editorial territory.

## THE PEOPLE DEBT — 100 figures, uncurated — 2026-06-11
## (figures100.py; four contact sheets, 25 each, 50 men / 50 women)

No props, no stories, no environments. Every figure seeded variation
across view (back/front/profile), build, stance, lean, weight, hair,
clothing, head pitch and turn. Shown as generated; nothing removed.

**The answer to the assignment's question: not yet — and the sheets
say exactly why.**

What holds at volume: the figures stand ON the ground; weight shifts
read; sex reads; posture variety reads; nothing is broken anatomy.
The floor of quality is far above where this corpus stood weeks ago.

What the volume exposes — the rig cannot hide across 100 bodies:

1. **One body type, repeated.** Everyone is slim and the same
   height. The parameter ranges were timid — the 501-14 lesson
   recommitted at scale (amplitudes polite = clones). No heavy
   bodies, no broad ones, no short ones, no old ones beyond a waist
   tweak. Humanity is wider than my priors.
2. **Profile is the weakest family.** Bowed backs read hunched or
   ambiguous; facing direction is sometimes unclear. Profile needs
   its own construction pass, as seated did.
3. **Faces vanish at distance.** Front-view features at contact-sheet
   scale are too light to register; feature weight must scale up as
   figures scale down.
4. **Feet are still ticks** on roughly a third of the figures.

Verdict: the debt is real, now measured, and itemized. People-volume
work continues with widened ranges (mass, height, age), a profile
construction pass, and scale-aware feature weights — in that order.

## OBSERVED PEOPLE — installment 1 of 40 — 2026-06-11
## (observed40_1.py; five individuals, two bus stops, sentence-first)

Census verdict accepted: stop varying parameters, start observing
people. Method: street-photography study (real, candid, unfashioned
people), one observation sentence written BEFORE each drawing, the
drawing in service of the sentence. Reconstructed, never traced.

OB1 — the woman whose pocketed fists push the whole jacket forward.
OB2 — the woman who stands perfectly vertical inside her coat.
OB3 — the man whose neck cranes past the shelter edge.
OB4 — the man whose parka erases his waist.
OB5 — the student whose body built itself around the bag strap.

**Result, visible at a glance: these five contain more human variety
than all one hundred generated figures combined.** No two could be
mistaken for each other; silhouette alone separates them. The
faculty's rule demonstrated in one comparison — human variety is not
invented, it is discovered. The sentence-first protocol is the
working method for the remaining 35: the sentence forces the
observation, and the observation forces the body.

Debts: the parka hood reads as a second head-bump; the craning man's
tipped chin is subtle; the vertical woman's heels-together feet are
mushy. All five stay. 35 remain.

## OBSERVED PEOPLE — installment 2: the consequence rule — 2026-06-11
## (observed40_2.py; sheet observed40_sheet2.png)

The faculty rule "what else would this change?" applied. Three
bodies solving problems, each drawn as a chain, the chain written as
the sentence.

**OB5v2 — the strap student, redrawn whole.** One bag, six
consequences: shoulder drops, neck tilts opposite to LEVEL the head
(bodies hide their adaptations — the level head over the oblique
neck is the key observation), ribs drift off the load, pelvis hikes,
weight settles left, the right foot half retires and turns out.
Against v1's single dropped shoulder, v2 reads as a body that has
adapted, not a body with a feature. The rule is real.

**OB6 — protecting warmth.** Arms crossed into a bar, shoulders
risen to eat the neck, chest hollowed, chin tucked, knees pressed,
hair fallen closed. Every hinge folded inward. Reads cold at a
glance.

**OB7 — saving energy (the post-leaner). Winner of the sheet.** The
post takes a third of him; the body hangs off the contact; the near
leg goes slack and crosses, toe down — the best single line of the
installment; hands in pockets complete the economy. The contact
point reorganizes the entire figure, which is the consequence rule
at maximum.

Finding: consequence-thinking produces visibly better figures than
feature-thinking. The drawings are starting to catch the sentences.
33 of 40 remain.
