# July Cover — AI Illustration Brief ("What grew.")

Art direction for a dedicated AI illustration pass. Generate to this brief,
then it comes back into the Hessentials system (placed, sized to the cover
slot, warm-tuned to our palette, judged against the Image Standard).

## The one job
Draw a **discovered Sonoran desert yard in late July**. Nothing arranged for
the viewer, nothing labeled, nothing making a point. The landscape itself
carries the essay. Reads first as *"a quiet, beautiful July yard."* Only after
reading does it become *"every object was evidence."* It rewards a second look;
it never explains itself on the first.

The essay is about **conditions**, not effort. No one made anything happen; the
yard answered. So: **no protagonist, no action, no gardener, no before/after
pair, nothing centered, nothing heroic.**

## Medium & style (non-negotiable)
- **Graphite / fine pencil illustration on warm cream paper.** It must read as
  *made* — a drawing that declares itself drawn. **Never photographic, never
  photoreal, never a render of a "real moment."**
- Hessentials restraint: **generous negative space** (sky/cream is ~70–80% of
  the frame), quiet, unhurried, 1990–2005 prestige-editorial calm.
- Observed, not decorative. Botanically believable plant forms — specific,
  seen — but economical. Confidence and restraint over detail-for-its-own-sake.
- Warm late-afternoon **July light**: warmth gathers low across the ground,
  the upper cream stays cooler. Hopeful, alive — *not* bleached or
  post-apocalyptic. Guiding feeling: **"we can be sad and happy."**

## Composition
- Portrait, **2:3** (cover slot). High, calm viewpoint looking across the yard.
- A gentle desert ground in the lower ~third; a huge open warm-cream sky above.
  A faint high horizon. **No dramatic slope, no ridge, no path/road** (a path
  implies a journey — agency — which we are avoiding).
- Evidence **scattered at varied depths**, none centered, none dominant. The
  eye should wander and make discoveries.

## The cast (observed Sonoran species + evidence objects)
Plants that are **present read thriving and alive** — that is "what grew."
Failure is shown by **absence**, never by dead brush:
- a **prickly pear / opuntia** (a few pads), established.
- a **creosote bush**, full and healthy.
- a low **brittlebush / desert-daisy** mound in flower (small pale flowers).
- an **ocotillo** or bunchgrass, a couple of quiet accents.
- small **volunteer tufts** here and there.

Evidence of intention, as absence:
- a **weathered nursery stake** standing over nothing, a small **blank hanging
  tag** — set in a **dry, empty planting basin** (a shallow ring, nothing in
  it). This is where someone tried. (Never a crossbar — must not read as a
  grave cross.)
- optionally a thin **drip line** across the ground near it.

Ground texture, subtle:
- a **scattered gravel / pebble field**, fine and quiet.
- faint **dry cracks / irrigation scars**. Restraint — texture, not busyness.

## Reading test (what it must do)
- Draw the **condition**, not the metaphor. The plants are evidence, not
  symbols. No single plant is the hero.
- **Refuses to signify.** Nothing announces its meaning. No object is staged as
  a Statement.
- The empty tagged stake in its dry basin + the thriving unplanned volunteers =
  the essay's turn (*"the question was never what you planted; it was what the
  ground would keep"*) — but felt, discovered, never spelled out.

## Hard negatives (do not include)
- No people, no figure, no hands, no gardener, no silhouette.
- No path, road, or trail. No signage or text (blank tag only).
- No photoreal / photographic look, no 3D render, no bright saturated color, no
  gradients-as-effect, no sunset spectacle.
- Not busy. When in doubt, fewer objects, more cream.

## Palette
Warm cream paper (near `#f5efe1`, warming to a soft gold low in the frame),
graphite greys for the line and light tonal shading. No other color, or the
faintest whisper only. Soft contrast.
