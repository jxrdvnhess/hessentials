## Session 1 — June 10, 2026
Source: The New Yorker, Cover Story archive (recent covers, viewed at thumbnail-to-medium scale)

**NY-01 · Mark Ulriksen, "Kings of New York" (May 25, 2026).**
Shown: one giant present-day Knick towering, screaming; a procession of historic Knicks at his waist. Omitted: opponent, court context. Implied: lineage culminating in now. Stance: celebratory, mythic. The scale distortion does all the work; nothing is left for the viewer to finish. Reads as poster. Describes a feeling rather than producing one.

**NY-02 · Kadir Nelson, "Plein Air" (May 18, 2026).**
Shown: a painter at a field easel in a park, dappled light, palette in hand. Omitted: the painting's subject — we can't see what he sees. Implied: the act of looking itself; who gets to be the painter rather than the painted. Stance: quiet, unhurried. The omission (his canvas faces away) is the engine. Viewer completes the picture he's making.

**NY-03 · Tomer Hanuka, "Spring Salutations" (Apr 27, 2026).**
Shown: park yoga, foreground body cropped to legs/feet inverted, second figure tying a shoe, skyline behind. Omitted: faces of the nearest figures. Implied: the park as shared stage; spring as bodily event. Stance: warm, slightly comic. Crop forces reconstruction; the viewer assembles the scene.

**NY-04 · Christoph Niemann, "West Fourth" (Apr 20, 2026).**
Shown: the W. 4th St. court; players rendered AS the chain-link mesh pattern — the fence texture constitutes the figures. Omitted: any rendering independent of the fence. Implied: the viewer's exact position — on the sidewalk, outside the cage, watching through it. Stance: observed, civic, affectionate. The means of viewing becomes the means of rendering. Form = viewpoint = meaning. Strongest of the set.

**NY-05 · R. Kikuo Johnson, "Meet-Cute" (Apr 13, 2026).**
Shown: dog in leopard-print stroller and toddler in stroller, face to face at knee height; adults cropped at the waist. Omitted: adult faces, adult world. Implied: the city's two kinds of "babies"; a whole demographic essay in one crop. Stance: sincere irony — comic premise, tenderly drawn. The crop IS the argument.

**NY-06 · Victoria Tentler-Krylov, "Parallel Lives" (Mar 30, 2026).**
Shown: street excavation, open pit, giant pipe, workers, pedestrians flowing around scaffolding; loose watercolor reportage. Omitted: nothing — density is the point. Implied: two cities operating in the same square yard. Stance: observed, on-site, journalistic. Carries thought through simultaneity rather than omission — a counter-data-point to the "omission" pattern.

First note, not yet a hypothesis: in the strongest of these, the VIEWER'S POSITION is encoded in the picture's form (NY-04 fence-as-render, NY-05 knee-height crop, NY-03 ground-level crop). The weakest (NY-01) positions the viewer nowhere — it addresses an audience, not a person standing somewhere.

## Session 2 — June 10, 2026
Sources: The Casual Optimist "Book Covers of Note, May 2026"; Pinterest search "museum exhibition poster design."

### Book jackets

**BK-01 · *Attention Seeking Behavior*, US (Michael Salu, Graywolf).** Salmon ground; a vertical strip of photographed skin splits the title apart. The interruption IS attention-seeking behavior — the cover performs its title. Meaning fully explicit, yet the thought lives in the form's misbehavior.

**BK-02 · *Attention Seeking Behaviour*, UK (Luke Bird, Peninsula).** Red display type whose strokes swell, droop, and gape — an O becomes an open mouth; letters needing to be looked at. Same book, same strategy, different instrument: type that performs neediness. Two designers independently chose enactment over depiction.

**BK-03 · *Carryout* (Kathleen Lynch, U. Iowa).** Night photograph, slightly elevated view: a lit corner takeout shop, interior fully legible (stools, OPEN neon, plants), title rendered as the shop's own neon sign in the dark above. The title placed INSIDE the world as signage. Observed register in photography; the shop as evidence of lives. Embodied viewpoint: across the street, at a window.

**BK-04 · Mendelsund's Pessoa series ×4 (New Directions, 2017–2026).** One archival portrait per volume, each defaced by a different mark system: thick black scribble-glasses (Reis), tangled colored contour (de Campos), scattered confetti dashes (Caeiro), posterized paint blots (Disquiet). Series logic: one man, many invented selves → one photo, many systems of interference. Discovered structural transposition (heteronymy = defacement grammar), sustained across a decade. Meaning is relational ACROSS covers — a 301 finding operating commercially.

**BK-05 · *Dog Days*, Emily LaBarge (Jared Bartman, Transit).** Victorian mourning photograph — veiled woman — with a pink crescent collaged over the face and pink stars burning through the black. Archive interrupted by tenderness. Explicit materials, no puzzle; the intelligence is tonal calibration (grief handled without grief-pose).

**BK-06 · *The Fifth Year*, Haushofer (Matt Dorfman, New Directions).** Torn paper scraps drifting across white like snow; type set among them. Material metaphor, quiet, no figure.

**BK-07 · *The Hill*, Harriet Clark (Na Kim, FSG).** Blue sky, green hill, tiny figure at the summit — and the hill is a human crown: brown hair, visible part, whorl. The double-read arrives a beat late and can't be unseen. Discovered transposition (hill = head), one move, fully committed. Strongest of the set.

**BK-08 · *Homebound*, Portia Elan (Miceli/Bacca, Scribner).** Painted storm sea dissolving into pixel-quilt blocks at one corner; two rendering systems colliding mid-image. The dissolution carries the thought (home/unhome, analog/digital). 

**BK-09 · *Honey* (designer t.b.c.).** Voluptuous groovy red display type "HONEY" on pink; below, a photographed real bee pinned through the thorax with a red entomology pin. Sweetness above, specimen violence below. The pin is the argument.

**MP-02 · "Toute une histoire!" (Musée de l'Armée).** Collection objects line-printed in blue; enormous red script title snaking through them. The script unifies disparate objects into one sentence — design does the curating.

## Session 3 — June 10, 2026
Sources: Pinterest searches (movie posters, album covers). Attack Vectors A and B from the Session 2 review.

### Movie posters

**MV-01 · *Jaws*, original one-sheet (Kastel painting, 1975).** Swimmer at the surface, oblivious; shark rising vertically, enormous, fully shown. The poster's engine is not the shark — it is the RELATION: scale gap, vertical axis, and the time it implies (the moment before). The viewer knows what the swimmer cannot; the image makes the viewer complicit. Thumbnail-proof, instant, global.

**MV-02 · *Jaws* fan remakes (×4, same screen).** Controlled comparison — same subject, same title, different selections. Frontal mouth close-ups, shark with a man standing on its fin, texture exercises. They keep the monster and lose the relation. Spectacle survives; dread does not. The difference between the original and the remakes is purely SELECTION — which is the fellowship's whole subject in one screen.

**MV-03 · *Alien*, original one-sheet (1979).** A cracked egg hovering in black, green light leaking, over an alien-skin lattice. No monster, no crew. "In space no one can hear you scream." Threat in potential form — the source, not the event.

**MV-04 · *Alien* variants (Giger creature portrait; Ripley-in-helmet fan posters).** Show the creature or the hero; description replaces dread.

Joint note on MV-01/MV-03: Jaws SHOWS its monster completely; Alien shows nothing. So concealment is not the shared engine. What they share: both stage an asymmetry — the viewer is positioned just before the event, holding knowledge the image's world doesn't have (the swimmer doesn't know; the egg hasn't opened). Participation = being placed in time, made complicit. This is the participation thread (S2 review, Obs. 3) in narrative form.

### Album covers

**AL-01 · Pink Floyd, *The Dark Side of the Moon* (Hipgnosis/Thorgerson, 1973).** A diagram: prism, beam, spectrum, black ground. No band, no scene, no mood imagery, no argument. It is not an announcement and not an essay — it is an EMBLEM: the face of a body of sound met hundreds of times across years. Its quality criterion is non-exhaustion: a diagram cannot be used up. Third function class confirmed (after announcement and argument): the sigil.

**AL-02 · Tyler, the Creator, *Flower Boy* (2017).** Painted surreal scene — boy in sunflower field, face hidden by a swarm of bees, golden sky. Mood-forward; one concealment gesture pins it. Answer forming for the professor's Q: yes, an image can succeed primarily through mood — but the strong mood images carry one structural decision (here: the hidden face) that keeps the atmosphere from being only atmosphere.

**AL-03 · Pusha T, *Daytona* (2018).** Torn black-and-white photograph revealing a second layer beneath. Material gesture as identity; tabloid energy.

**AL-04 · Template covers ("SONG NAME / ARTIST NAME" mockups).** The null class again, in a second genre: album-cover grammar (eye + clouds + condensed type) applied to no particular music. Decoration = the genre's moves without the genre's subject.

Sources: Pinterest (political/propaganda posters; Penguin Great Ideas). Vector C + first visual-systems observation.

### Political persuasion (Vector C — can excellence survive directness?)

**PP-01 · Flagg, "I Want YOU" (1917).** Pointing finger, locked eyes, second-person address. Total directness, zero ambiguity — and excellent. The participation is grammatical: the image conscripts the viewer as its subject. You are not shown a message; you are cast in a role.

**PP-02 · "War is good business — invest your son" (Vietnam protest).** Pietà photograph + sardonic copy. Fully direct, but the viewer must perform the inversion (read "good business" as obscenity). Cognitive participation via irony; the poster doesn't work until you complete it.

**PP-03 · "Are we good citizens?" (Lincoln Memorial, red/black).** The question mark is the engine — direct address as interrogation; the viewer supplies the answer.

**PP-04 · Black Panther "All Power to the People."** Slogan + panther sigil. The emblem function appears in politics: a badge designed for repetition — marches, walls, jackets. Non-exhaustion again (cf. AL-01).

**PP-05 · "Be careful what you say" (Hitler caricature perched on telephone wires).** Even propaganda reaches for discovered transposition when it wants thought: eavesdropper = bird on the wires.

**PP-06 · Weak pole: "UNITED" flag montage; "Wake Up America" allegory; "You Are Obsolete" constructivist pastiche.** Direct declarations addressed to no one in particular; agreement assumed rather than produced. The flags display unity instead of conscripting anyone into it.

Provisional answer to the priority question: excellence survives directness when the image assigns the viewer a role — conscript (PP-01), ironist (PP-02), answerer (PP-03), carrier (PP-04). It fails when the image declares at nobody. Participation taxonomy gains a political type: **conscription (second-person grammar)**.

## Session 6 — June 10, 2026 — ASSIGNMENT 5A: THE NO-ROLE ATTACK
Corpus hostile to participation: Morandi still lifes ×4, Hammershøi interiors ×3, Hasui shin-hanga prints ×10, Friedrich *Monk by the Sea* (1 work, 4 reproductions), Saul Leiter photographs ×6, plus a Morandi-imitation null set. 24+ works. Rules observed: no new participation categories; counterexamples protected; dual statements.

**NR-01–04 · Morandi bottles.** Objects huddled at center, dusty close-keyed color, edges that tremble. No task, no address, no narrative, no asymmetry, no conscription. Strong. What is present instead: the visible record of prolonged looking — the same bottles painted hundreds of times, each pass slower. The force seems to sit in the OBJECT AS DEPOSIT of the painter's attention.

**NR-05–07 · Hammershøi interiors.** Sun parallelograms on a bare floor; dim furniture against grey panels. Empty of event. The room has been watched, not staged. Same finding as Morandi: no role, but immense recorded patience.

**NR-08–17 · Hasui snow prints.** Harbor snow, torii in snow, temple steps, night villages. Many include one small figure (umbrella-bearer, walker) — honest check: the figure is scale and habitation, not surrogate; the print does not ask to be entered. Dominant experience: weather rendered as patient labor — thousands of carved flecks. Commercial objects (shin-hanga export prints) made for dwelling, not announcement. Commitment finding (CB-05) extends: weather drawn as if true.

**NR-18 · Friedrich, *Monk by the Sea*.** Tiny upright figure, void sky at 80% of canvas. Strain case in the OTHER direction: the monk half-assigns a place (Rückenfigur logic at minimum strength). Logged as boundary object between the engines, not absorbed by either.

**NR-19–24 · Saul Leiter.** Figures through fogged glass, snow on a windshield from inside, red umbrella against graffiti, his garden under snow from his own window. Critical dissociation: Leiter gives the viewer a radical PLACE (behind this glass, in this warmth, at this window) and no TASK whatsoever. Place and task come apart cleanly.

**NR-25 · Morandi-imitation class (renders, palette pins).** "Morandi-style" sphere-cube-vase compositions, glossy, instant. Quietness as template. The null class has a contemplative variant: presence can be counterfeited, and the counterfeit fails exactly as templates fail — the moves without the thing being true. (For Hessentials this one matters.)

### Assignment 5A finding — OUTCOME C (reported immediately per rules)
A second engine appears to exist. Participation (task-assignment) explains one family: editorial argument, persuasion, suspense, conscription. It does NOT explain Morandi, Hammershøi, Hasui, or Leiter, and per Rule 1 no new participation category has been minted to absorb them.
- Surface statement: quiet images slow the viewer down and ask nothing.
- Mechanism statement (candidate): these images are RECORDS OF ATTENTION PAID. The maker's prolonged attention is legible in the artifact — pace of marks, repetition, patience — and the viewer's attention entrains to the recorded tempo. The viewer doesn't complete, invert, or answer; the viewer receives. Call this engine PRESENCE (per the assignment's watch list), pending attack.
- Clean dissociations now on record: Leiter = place without task (strong). Uncle Sam = task without place (strong). Morandi = neither (strong). Participation and place are therefore separable variables, and neither is universal.

**VS-01 · Penguin Great Ideas (Pearson-designed series, ~100+ vols).** Fixed constraint: white/two-color grounds, no images, title-as-image typography. Per-volume invention sourced from each text's own era and temper — Kropotkin's title drips like painted agitprop, Veblen sits in engraved wallpaper, Perec stacks bookish slabs. Coherence mechanism (observed, not concluded): the constraint is held constant while variation is REQUIRED to come from the book's substance, not the designer's mood. Same structure as Mendelsund's Pessoa series (BK-04) at 25× scale. Note for later: this is also the Pinterest-recognizable structure of the New Yorker covers (fixed masthead/format, roaming register) — three systems now share it.

## Session 7 — June 10, 2026 — ASSIGNMENT 5B: THE PRESENCE ATTACK
Groups: A candidate presence (Rothko ×4 repros; Agnes Martin and real Wyeth NOT adequately observed — corpus gap, queued); B counterfeit presence (slow-living illustration ×8, decor-Rothko ×3, faux-Wyeth AI dusk-house ×1); C ornament threats (Morris + Persian carpets ×10). ~26 new works.

Result: participation degrades least (prediction confirmed); presence-through-facture degrades hard; presence-through-composition is robust. The facture claim is bounded, not general.

### Assignment 5B result — presence v1 destroyed, v2 rebuilt from the rubble
"Recorded attention" dies under canon-gravity: a story about the maker's biography, unverifiable from the artifact, suspiciously co-extensive with prestige knowledge.
- Surface statement: quiet images use quiet vocabulary.
- Mechanism statement (presence v2): **the image repays the second minute.** Strong contemplative work carries a density of ordered-but-unresolved relation that keeps yielding under sustained viewing; counterfeits front-load. Checkable by a viewer who knows no names — applied this session, it sorted Group B from Groups A/C without provenance.
- "Can attention be faked?": the vocabulary can be faked; the density cannot be faked without doing the work — at which point it isn't fake. Counterfeit detection = ask what the second minute returns.
- The maker's long attention remains the usual CAUSE of density; density is the operative CRITERION. Cause and criterion separated.

Open question, deliberately NOT formalized (solvent risk): participation assigns work; presence v2 repays volunteered work. Both concern viewer work — assigned vs offered. Parked for the professor.

### Assignment 5C result — presence v2 REGIONALIZED
Excellence without second-minute return exists (marks, posters, conscription images). Three temporal classes observed, mapping cleanly to function:
1. **Instant-complete** (marks, emblems): all value at 3s; criterion = durability across encounters and scales.
2. **Two-stage** (editorial compression, argument covers): 3s hook + engineered 60s payoff; designed exhaustion by 3min.
3. **Slow-release** (contemplative work, ornament): minimal 3s; continuous yield under dwelling.
The image's JOB sets its temporal contract (H4v2 strengthened again). Presence v2 survives only as the criterion of class 3 — a regional law, exactly as the assignment's Outcome test specified.
Necessity-suspicion sightings (observation only, per professor): the swoosh and the $-lawn both read as "could not be otherwise"; the derivative badges read as "could be a hundred ways." Counted, not formalized.

## Session 9 — June 10, 2026 — ASSIGNMENT 5D: THE ARBITRARINESS ATTACK
Method note: run on strong-neighbor pairs ALREADY IN the observed corpus — the only way to honor the "no weak imitations" rule without inventing counterfactuals. Real pairs available: *Attention Seeking Behaviour* US vs UK (same book, two strong covers); Alien egg vs Giger creature portrait (both real, both strong); Jaws original vs strongest variants; Morandi vs Morandi (the series IS a set of strong alternatives); swoosh vs other canonical marks. Class C (survives-replacement-by-design): template classes already held.

**Test 1 · ASB US vs UK.** Same book. Both excellent. Excellence SURVIVES replacement — at the device level. Skin-strip interruption vs needy letterforms: different devices, same underlying relation (the title performs itself). What was selected: a device. What persisted across both editions: the relation. Loss under swap: none — because both editions kept the relation.

**Test 2 · Alien egg vs Giger creature.** Both strong standalone. Swap degrades the SYSTEM: with the creature shown, "in space no one can hear you scream" loses its engine — the withheld threat.
