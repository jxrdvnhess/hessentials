## D-1 · THE VENUE FIXES THE CONTRACT; THE CONTRACT FIXES THE CRITERIA
Entered June 10, 2026, after fifteen sessions, eight named attack assignments (5A–5H), and a formal prosecution. First and only entry.

**Text.** A work's quality criteria are set by the contract its venue, commission, and declared register fix before the work exists. The advertised payment must arrive. Payment schedules differ by class — instant-complete work owes durability across encounters and scales; two-stage work owes an immediate read plus an engineered second read; slow-release work owes continued yield under sustained attention. Contracts stack: honoring the advertised contract and exceeding it is the structure of excellence. Open refusal of a contract is honest. Covert payment failure is counterfeit.

**Clauses required by the prosecution, now part of the text.**
1. *Readability:* the operative contract must be statable by a third party from venue, commission, and declared register alone, before reception is known. Demonstrated at entry: the London roundel's contract (identifiable at distance, at speed, at any size) was fixed by transport commissioning decades before any judgment of its success — observed today from archival source.
2. *Class pre-commitment:* a new payment-schedule class may be added only with a falsifiable criterion distinct from the existing three, stated before any anomaly is absorbed by it.
3. *Domain boundary:* valid only for venue-bound work, whose obligations pre-exist it. Silent on work whose subject is the contract system itself.

**Evidence base at entry.** 162 works across nine genres; emblem class now anchored by primary-source observation (roundel, ~117 years of deposit; I♥NY, 48 years, surviving documented unlicensed-counterfeit pressure; DSOTM; Panther). Re-venue prediction supported by same-day pilot only (Morandi sorted from imitations under dwell, not in scroll grid) — the controlled experiment remains owed.

**Standing falsifiers — any one kills or reopens the entry.**
- A contract unreadable ex ante by a third party in a venue-bound case the doctrine claims (Count 1 returning in force).
- A fourth class minted without its pre-committed criterion (Count 4 realized).
- A strong venue-bound work whose advertised payment demonstrably never arrives and which is NOT felt as counterfeit by provenance-blind viewers.
- The full re-venue experiment failing (same image, venue changed, quality judgment unchanged).

**Residual obligations carried into the entry:** NPS arrowhead and Apple still unobserved from primary sources; the controlled re-venue experiment; contract readability tested on a multi-contract venue (the New Yorker problem from the prosecution's Count 1).
