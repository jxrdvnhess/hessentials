# DOCTRINE DRAFT + PROSECUTION — Assignment 5H, June 10, 2026

## Step 1 — The candidate, as it would appear in the Doctrine Log

> **D-1 (candidate). The venue fixes the contract; the contract fixes the criteria.**
> A work's quality criteria are set by the contract its venue, commission, and declared register fix before the work exists. The advertised payment must arrive. Payment schedules differ by class: instant-complete work owes durability across encounters; two-stage work owes an immediate read plus an engineered second read; slow-release work owes continued yield under sustained attention. Contracts stack: honoring the advertised contract and exceeding it is the structure of excellence. Open refusal of a contract is honest. Covert payment failure is counterfeit.
> Valid only for venue-bound work — work whose obligations pre-exist it. Silent on work whose subject is the contract system itself.

## Step 2–3 — The case against admission

**Count 1: Circularity (residual).** The venue clause does not finish the job, because venues under-determine contracts. A New Yorker cover must announce, argue, and charm at once — and the record shows the prosecutor's point: the fellowship scored Ulriksen against a discovery standard and Kalman's bulldog against a deadpan-observation standard, same venue, no rule deciding which test applied. Where the venue fixes several contracts, taste chooses among them, and the success→job inference re-enters disguised as emphasis. The doctrine stands only if the contract is readable by a third party — from venue, commission, and declared register — WITHOUT seeing the work's reception. The current draft asserts this readability; it has not demonstrated it. HIT.

**Count 2: Domain boundary — principled or convenient?** The boundary was written the day conceptual art threatened; the timing is suspicious by the fellowship's own standards. The defense — obligations either pre-exist the work or they don't, an independent property — is real but leaks: Sendak's moonlit spread and Friedrich are venue-bound (a book page, a salon wall) yet the venue does not obviously fix the criteria the fellowship used on them. The boundary holds between venue-art and anti-venue art; it blurs within venues that license multiple registers. PARTIAL HIT — the boundary is principled at the outer wall, undemonstrated in the interior rooms.

**Count 3: Predictive power.** The doctrine does predict beyond the folk version ("good work is good for its genre"): (1) cross-genre misjudgment is systematic and directional — the judge imports the home genre's test (confirmed in-house, dove badge); (2) re-venuing changes quality without changing pixels — Morandi as scroll content degrades, the same file in a dwelling context does not (untested as experiment); (3) counterfeit detection by payment audit works provenance-blind (tested once, 5B); (4) mark redesigns fail when they trade deposit for vocabulary (untested against redesign history). Two of four predictions are untested. A doctrine may not be admitted on predictions it has not yet risked. HIT — not against truth, against admission timing.

**Count 4: Compression.** Three temporal classes from ~160 works, one day, one observer. The class system is the doctrine's engine, and the fellowship's own error class 2 (taxonomy degeneration) describes exactly how it will fail: a fourth class minted to absorb the next anomaly. The emblem class already received three special treatments; the record reads as convergence, but the fellowship wanted convergence by then. Admission requires a pre-commitment: a new class may be added only with a falsifiable criterion distinct from the existing three, stated before the anomaly is absorbed. The draft lacks this clause. HIT.

**The marks debt, entered as prejudicial circumstance.** The emblem class — load-bearing for deposit, for the counterfeit exception, for the temporal taxonomy — rests on DSOTM, the Panther, and a swoosh observed mostly through merchandise. The class that bent three theories is the class with the thinnest primary evidence. No admission while this stands.

## Step 4 — Verdict

**REVISE + TRIAL EXTENSION.** Not Reject: the core survived genuine prosecution — Counts 1–4 wound the draft's completeness, not its mechanism, and the strongest available attack (circularity) was answered at the outer wall. Not Enter: two revisions are required in the text — (a) a contract-readability clause: the operative contract must be statable by a third party from venue, commission, and declared register alone, before reception is known; (b) the class pre-commitment clause above. And one debt must be paid in evidence: the canonical marks from primary sources, plus at least one run of the re-venue experiment (prediction 2), which is cheap and decisive.

The Doctrine Log remains empty tonight. The candidate exists, the prosecution is on file, and the conditions for entry are named and finite.
