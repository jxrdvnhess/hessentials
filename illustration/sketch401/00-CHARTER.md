## Core question

What makes one image worth choosing over another? Why does one image carry thought while another merely describes?

## The path here

101 — structure precedes appearance. 201 — observation outranks schema. 301 — meaning is relational. 401 — selection: if meaning emerges from relationships, what makes an image worth choosing?

## Research independence

The fellowship is not authorized to defend existing Hessentials doctrine. A finding that contradicts Hessentials is more valuable than one that supports it. No doctrine is exempt.

## The eight questions

1. What separates editorial illustration from decoration?
2. What separates editorial illustration from explanation?
3. What separates ambiguity from confusion?
4. What causes an image to trust its viewer?
5. What causes an image to feel literary?
6. What causes an image to feel observed rather than designed?
7. What causes an image to feel emotionally intelligent?
8. What characteristics repeatedly appear in the strongest editorial images?

## Method

Collect → observe (observation precedes interpretation) → hypothesize → attack (seek failure, not support) → refine → transfer test (unseen images, existing Hessentials work, hypothetical covers).

## Corpus minimums

500 editorial illustrations · 250 magazine covers · 250 book covers · 100 museum/exhibition graphics · 100 Hessentials-adjacent. Numbers reduce anecdote; they are not the goal.

## Working documents

- `01-research-journal.md` — raw observations, by session
- `02-pattern-log.md` — potential discoveries
- `03-counterexample-log.md` — failed hypotheses (the most important document)
- `04-doctrine-log.md` — only findings that survive repeated attack
- `05-corpus-ledger.md` — running count and source record

## Midpoint trigger

Report when three doctrine-level findings survive transfer testing, OR the corpus exceeds 600 analyzed works — whichever first.

## Completion

A doctrine that predicts successful and unsuccessful editorial images before reading accompanying text, survives counterexample testing, transfers to unseen work, and generates practical selection criteria.
