# COUNTEREXAMPLE LOG — failed hypotheses

The most important document. Every entry: the hypothesis as stated, the work that broke it, and what the break teaches.

## CX-01 · H1 v1 broken — same day it was proposed
Hypothesis: "Strong editorial images encode the viewer's bodily position; weak ones address an audience from nowhere."
Broken by: NY-25 (Flatiron as icebreaker — viewpoint from nowhere a person stands, still strong), NY-13 (Ana Juan's dog taxonomy — catalog view, still strong), NY-18 (Chast calendar — no viewpoint exists, still strong).
Teaches: I generalized from the register I personally favor (observed/street-level) to all editorial images. The corpus contains at least three working registers — observed, metaphoric, taxonomic/formal — and a law true of one is not a law of the genre. Watch for this error class: mistaking a register preference for a quality criterion. (This is also a live risk in Hessentials selection work.)

## CX-02 · H2 v1 ("the meaning is never written") — broken as stated
Hypothesis: strong images leave their meaning unstated; writing the meaning kills the image.
Broken by: BK-01 and BK-02 (*Attention Seeking Behaviour*, US & UK — the meaning IS the title, printed at full size, and both covers are strong because the form performs it); MP-03 (Gropius — "modernism breaks with the past" stated by physically tearing the sheet); MP-04 (Monet — pure announcement, institutionally excellent).
Teaches: explicitness is not the variable. The variable is whether the viewer is given work that matters — feeling the type misbehave, completing the tear, crossing from sweet type to pinned bee. NY-07 (food trucks) fails not because its satire is written but because reading the labels is the END of the encounter; nothing remains to do. Replacement direction (per S1 review, not yet formalized): required participation, not concealment.

## CX-03 · H4 v1 (strict functional relativism) — damaged by Type A attack, refined to v2
Hypothesis as stated: quality criteria are functions of the image's job (implying one job, one test).
Damaged by: Jaws and Alien one-sheets — announcements that are simultaneously arguments and mood objects, and that outlive their announcing function by decades.
Teaches: function defines the floor, not the ceiling. Also repeats the S1/S2 error structure in a new costume: "job" was treated as a surface category (genre) when the causal variable may be which TESTS an image passes, regardless of genre. Genre is descriptive; the test-stack is causal. (Methodological pattern from the S2 review — descriptive first explanation, causal second — confirmed a third time.)

## CX-04 · H5 universalized — bounded by Vector D
Hypothesis (as it was drifting): asymmetry of knowledge is the participation mechanism of narrative images.
Bounded by: Sendak's rumpus spreads — fully symmetric knowledge (reader, Max, monsters), no moment-before, strong anyway. Carried instead by invitation: the child reader occupies Max.
Teaches: H5 is the engine of SUSPENSE, one narrative register among several. Also: the fix ("add invitation to the taxonomy") is itself suspect — see meta-method entry on taxonomy degeneration. CB-04 (moonlit walking monster) is logged as a genuinely unabsorbed case: strong, quiet, and assigning no clear role. The professor's harder question — are there strong images that assign no role whatsoever? — now has a live candidate.

## CX-05 · Presence v1 ("recorded attention") — destroyed by its own attack round (5B)
Hypothesis: the maker's prolonged attention is legible in the artifact and entrains the viewer.
Destroyed by: canon-gravity analysis (the claim is co-extensive with prestige knowledge; unverifiable from the artifact) + the Rothko thumbnail test (real vs decor-fake indistinguishable at small scale — if attention were legible, it should not vanish with resolution while the prestige judgment persists) + Hasui (presence surviving thumbnail through composition, contradicting the facture mechanism as stated).
Rebuilt as v2: the image repays the second minute — density of ordered-but-unresolved relation, checkable without provenance. Cause (attention) separated from criterion (density).
Teaches: fourth instance of the error pattern, in a new costume — v1 explained the artifact by the BIOGRAPHY behind it (descriptive of origin) rather than by structure in it (causal in viewing). Origin stories are surface vocabulary too.

## CX-06 · Presence v2 universality — broken by the Speed Attack (5C)
Hypothesis (as it threatened to expand): strong images repay the second minute.
Broken by: the Nike swoosh and the emblem class — zero second-minute return, excellent for decades; the dwell-corpus had selected for dwell-time exactly as the professor suspected.
Survives as: the regional criterion of slow-release work (temporal class 3 of 3). The second-minute test remains the best counterfeit detector WITHIN that class, and the cross-encounter durability test serves the emblem class. Note: v2 was always closer to a thermometer than an engine (professor's second-pass warning) — 5C confirms it measures one temporal contract among three.
