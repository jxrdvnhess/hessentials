# MIDTERM — SELECTION UNDER CONSTRAINT
June 10, 2026

Scope declared: the full A–F pipeline is run at depth on ONE text, *Half revealed.* (read from source, src/app/half-revealed/page.tsx). Running five texts × twenty concepts would have produced a hundred shallow justifications; the exam evaluates selection, and the first selection was the scope. The same pipeline applies to the remaining texts in subsequent sittings.

The text's spine: January is imagination, June is evidence. The house shows where the year went. Not an audit. Things survived without protection: the route feet learned, the cup that migrated and stayed, the chair sat in for the wrong reason, the corner that stayed cleared and stayed empty.

## PART B — Elimination (fellowship findings only)

- **5, 6 (split/diptych) — KILLED.** Before/after is a received device; it transfers to any change-story without loss (H6 transfer test). Worse: a side-by-side comparison IS an audit, and the text explicitly refuses the audit. The device contradicts the essay.
- **13 (January calendar) — KILLED.** Writes the meaning onto the image (NY-07 lesson: reading the label ends the encounter). Explanation, not discovery.
- **14 (half-developed room) — KILLED.** A metaphor about rendering, i.e., about the image itself — an Image-with-a-message. Violates refuses-to-signify; the device transfers to any reveal-story.
- **17 (two doors) — KILLED.** Binary contrast = audit grammar again, miniaturized.
- **3 (table set for no one) — KILLED.** Staged absence is performed melancholy — the grief pose. The register is warm melancholy FELT, not arranged. Counterfeit risk: advertises feeling it did not find.
- **15 (house from outside) — KILLED.** The essay's fixed viewpoint is inside, walking at dusk (H1v2: the observed register requires the body the text supplies). The exterior view is another essay.
- **19 (back-view figure) — KILLED.** Right register, wrong slot: the anonymous back-view man is the About page's load-bearing relation. System coherence (one intelligence, many rooms) forbids reusing a room's relation elsewhere.
- **7, 20 (dust topography, touched/untouched shelf) — KILLED, and the reason matters.** The text already states this mechanism ("dust settles on the surfaces you stopped touching"). An image that restates the text's own metaphor leaves no gap between shown and meant (H2): the reader crosses nothing. This sharpened the rule for everything below: a stated ITEM may be used; the image must contribute an unstated DISCOVERY about it.
- **8 (plant past stake) — KILLED.** Stated item, and the only available discovery (growth passing a plan-line) transfers to any plans-outgrown essay. Fails H6 where 2/4/9 pass.
- **16 (bookmark at p. 30) — KILLED.** True discovery, weak weld: abandoned-book anecdote transfers freely. H6.
- **11 (blinds light) — KILLED.** Atmosphere with zero structural decisions — the mood-needs-one-pin rule (Flower Boy); unpinned quietness is the templated-quietness counterfeit class.
- **10 (doorway at dusk) — KILLED as standalone.** It is a point of view, not a concept; absorbed into the finalists' art direction.
- **18 (table surface record) — KILLED at the bubble.** Same mechanism class as 4 (time as residue) with a weaker weld: "real history" is general; the cup's migration-then-settlement is the essay's precise argument.

## PART C — Finalists

**F1 · The cleared corner, still empty (1).**
Shown: a corner, baseboard, floor, long light, nothing else. Omitted: whatever was meant to happen there. Load-bearing relation: emptiness held against intention — the omission is the subject. Contract proposed: slow-release; the image asks to be sat with. Risks: at three seconds it reads as nothing (venue is a scrolling page — the floor contract may go unpaid); dignity of emptiness collapses easily into bleakness.

**F2 · The chair's wear faces the wrong way (2).**
Shown: one chair, angled toward the window; seat and arm wear oriented toward the room. Omitted: the sitter, the reason. Load-bearing relation: bought-for vs used-for, recorded in fabric. Contract: two-stage — chair first, contradiction second. Risks: wear-direction is hard to draw legibly without exaggeration; exaggeration tips it into caption-logic (ambiguity → confusion, charter Q3).

**F3 · The cup and its trail of rings (4).**
Shown: a cup at rest in morning light; faint ring-ellipses crossing the counter, spacing tightening, ending beneath it. Omitted: hands, figure, steam, the window. Load-bearing relation: migration recorded as residue, then settlement — "survived without your protection" in physical form. The trail is NOT in the text; the cup is. Stated item, unstated discovery. Contract: two-stage — quiet object at a glance; after the essay, the rings count mornings. Risks: rings read as stains (sloppiness, not record); trail reads as clutter at thumbnail.

**F4 · The worn path on the floor plan (9).**
Shown: an architect's plan; one route through it darkened by feet. Omitted: the house itself, the walker. Load-bearing relation: June's evidence written onto January's document — the two halves of the essay on one sheet. Contract: two-stage, fast and legible. Risks: diagram vocabulary advertises explanation (infographic contract) in a Practice slot whose register is lived, not designed; cleverness reads as Statement — teeth migrating into décor.

**F5 · The light sitting in the chair (12).**
Shown: empty chair, evening light occupying the seat. Omitted: sitter; light source. Load-bearing relation: light as the room's last occupant — the dusk walkthrough condensed. Contract: slow-release with one pin (light-as-occupant). Risks: one step from atmospheric stock; the pin must carry everything or it joins the counterfeit-quietness class.

## PART D — Selection

**F3. The cup and its trail of rings.**

## PART E — Art direction

- **Composition.** Horizontal counter edge low in frame. Cup right of center, at rest. The ring trail enters from frame-left: ellipses faint, irregular spacing wide at first, tightening as they approach the cup — months compressing into habit — ending in the one darker ring beneath it. Generous negative space above; the wall holds nothing.
- **Point of view.** Standing height, eyes slightly above the counter, the position of someone in the kitchen early, holding nothing. No tilt, no drama.
- **Scale.** The cup small against the field. Restraint is the container; the trail does the feeling.
- **Subject.** A plain cup — no pattern, no logo, no character. The subject is not the cup; it is the record the cup left.
- **Light.** Long, low, horizontal June light through sealed glass; late-light register, interior membrane. No open window anywhere in frame (Houston truth). Light source trusted, not shown.
- **Omission strategy.** No figure, no hands, no steam (steam is slow-living token vocabulary — the adjacent counterfeit), no second object. The unwritten layer — that the rings are months — is left entirely to the reader returning from the essay.
- **Rendering expectations.** Graphite, line-forward (faces-rule generalized: line as carrier, no rendered-tone mush). Ring ellipses geometrically calm so they read as record, not spill. Edges feathered to #f8f6f3 per the homepage remap. Trail held one value lighter than the cup's resident ring.
- **Failure risks.** (1) Rings read as stains: control with ellipse regularity and value discipline. (2) Trail invisible at thumbnail: acceptable — the venue's first read only owes "cup, morning, quiet"; the second stage is owed to the reader, not the scroller. (3) Preciousness: if the cup acquires charm, the image starts performing; keep it plain.

## PART F — Defense

This concept survived the other nineteen because every fellowship finding that killed a rival leaves it standing. Its load is carried by a relation, not a device, and the relation fails the transfer test in exactly the way strong work does: a trail of rings ending under a settled cup is not movable to another essay — it is "migrated every morning and stayed," this text's argument, as residue. It honors the rule the eliminations sharpened: the text names the cup, but the rings are the image's own contribution — the gap between shown and meant stays open for the reader to cross, where the dust concepts closed it. It pays the venue's actual contract: an article-header slot owes a two-stage schedule, a quiet first read for the scroller and a second payment for the reader who comes back down the page changed by the essay — and the rings only become mornings on that second pass. It refuses all three counterfeit vocabularies adjacent to it: the staged table's performed melancholy, the diptych's audit grammar, the steam-and-sunbeam tokens of templated quietness. And it keeps the house discipline that predates the fellowship but that the fellowship independently re-derived: evidence over interpretation, discoveries over declarations, the condition rather than the setting. What remains in the frame is what the essay says June is: not the intention, and not the verdict — the record.
