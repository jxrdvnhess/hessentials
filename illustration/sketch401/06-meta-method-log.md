# META-METHOD LOG — recurring errors made by the fellowship itself

Created at the professor's direction after Session 3. The investigation studies editorial images AND how it arrives at false conclusions about them. Surface-first error is the established class; new error types get logged here as they appear.

## The descriptive-before-causal pattern (confirmed 3×)

| Session | Surface explanation (wrong) | Causal explanation (better) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Strong images encode viewer position (register feature) | Embodied viewpoint is the entry price of one register; I preferred that register |
| 2 | Strong images never write their meaning (explicitness) | Strong images require participation; explicitness is orthogonal |
| 3 | Quality criteria follow genre (genre) | Quality criteria follow the test-stack an image passes; genre only sets the floor |

Working rule derived: **any hypothesis stated in surface vocabulary (register, explicitness, genre, medium, style) is provisional by default.** Before promoting anything, restate it in mechanism vocabulary (what the viewer is asked to do; what the image must survive; what relation carries the load). If it can't be restated, it isn't understood yet.

## Confirmed error class 2 — taxonomy degeneration (Session 5)
When the participation taxonomy met resistance (Sendak's symmetric-knowledge rumpus), the available move was to mint a new participation type ("invitation/identification") and declare the hypothesis saved. A taxonomy that grows a new category every time it meets a counterexample explains everything and predicts nothing. Working rule: **a new category may only be added if it makes a falsifiable prediction the old categories didn't.** Otherwise the counterexample stays unabsorbed in the log (as CB-04 now does).

## Dual-statement requirement (per S4 review, now standing)
Every new hypothesis gets two statements at birth — Surface version (what appears to be happening) and Mechanism version (what might actually be happening). Example, Session 5 sincerity finding:
- Surface: children's illustration works without irony.
- Mechanism: commitment is a rendering discipline — the fictional thing drawn with the observational seriousness owed to a real thing ("drawn as if true"); the viewer's belief tracks the draftsman's, not the subject's plausibility.

## Confirmed error class 3 — origin stories as surface vocabulary (Session 7 / 5B)
Presence v1 explained the artifact by the biography behind it ("attention was paid") rather than by structure in it. Origin claims feel causal but are descriptive of provenance, not of viewing — and they import canon-gravity wholesale (knowing it's a Rothko substitutes for seeing it). Working rule: **a mechanism must be checkable by a viewer who knows no names.** The second-minute test passes this; "recorded attention" did not.

## Watch list addition (per 5E review, Observation 2) — observer-contract errors
Judging a work against a contract it never claimed: register preference as quality (CX-01), explicitness as weakness (CX-02), genre as function (CX-03), merchandise as failed mark (5E). The judge supplies the contract; the error is in the supplying. Selection doctrine must model observer failure, not only image failure. Watch-list status — not yet formalized.
