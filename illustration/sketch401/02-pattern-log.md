## H1 — Encoded viewer position · REFINED (v2)
v1 (broken, see Counterexample Log): "Strong editorial images encode the viewer's bodily position; weak ones address an audience from nowhere."
v2: **Images that claim the observed register need an embodied viewpoint** (NY-04 fence mesh = sidewalk; NY-05 knee-height; NY-15 behind the glancer; NY-21 passerby below the stoop). Images operating in the metaphoric or taxonomic register (NY-25 Flatiron-icebreaker, NY-13 dog catalog, NY-18 calendar) carry thought without one. Viewpoint is the price of admission for "observed," not a universal law.
Next attack: non-New-Yorker corpora; book jackets rarely have a viewpoint — do any feel observed anyway?

## H2 — The meaning is never written · UNDER ATTACK
The strong images leave a gap between what is shown and what is meant, and the viewer crosses it (Nelson's turned-away canvas; Johnson's cropped adults; Kroik's shared glance; Martin's crowd/field ratio). The weakest image in the set (NY-07 food trucks) writes its meaning onto the picture in words — nothing left to cross. Density without omission (NY-06) still works because the gap lives between the two simultaneous cities, not in missing material — so the gap is semantic, not material.
Risk: tautology ("good images imply rather than state"). Needs operational form before promotion: can I locate the gap, name its two sides, in every strong image — and fail to in every weak one?

## H3 — Discovered transposition vs. received emphasis · UNDER ATTACK
The engine of many strong covers is ONE transposition, fully committed: chef seasons the sidewalk (NY-12); Flatiron is an icebreaker (NY-25); players are fence-mesh (NY-04); dogs dress as their owners (NY-13). The transposition is DISCOVERED — a real correspondence between two real logics, found not invented. The weak cover's device is RECEIVED — giant-hero scale (NY-01), stock emphasis conventions that signal importance instead of finding likeness.
Note honestly: this converges with existing Hessentials doctrine ("discoveries, not declarations"). Charter forbids treating convergence as confirmation — the attack must run on corpora with no Hessentials gravity: museum posters, foreign-language covers, mid-market trade jackets.

## H3 — Discovered transposition · SURVIVED FIRST TRANSFER (book jackets), DOMAIN BOUNDED (museum posters)
Session 2 transfer test. Book jackets: survived strongly — *The Hill* (hill = head), Mendelsund's Pessoa series (heteronymy = defacement grammar), *Honey* (sweet type / pinned specimen), *Carryout* (title = the shop's own neon). The engine transfers outside magazines.
Museum posters: mostly ABSENT — and the posters are not worse for it. MP-04 (Monet/Marmottan) is excellent with no conceit at all. Refinement: **transposition is an engine for images that must carry an argument. Images whose job is announcement and authority (museum posters) don't need it and rarely use it.** The hypothesis is true of a function, not of images in general.
Exception inside posters: MP-03 (Gropius torn sheet) — when an exhibition has a thesis, enactment returns. Predicts: thesis-exhibitions use editorial devices; collection-shows use authority devices. Test on more posters.

## H4 (new) — Quality criteria are functions of the image's job · PROPOSED
Generalizes the session-1 methodological discovery. Register preference ≠ quality criterion (S1); now: genre function defines what "strong" means. The Monet poster is strong as announcement and would be weak as a New Yorker cover; *The Hill* would be a bad museum poster (it upstages the artifact). A selection doctrine that ignores the image's job will misjudge across genres.
Attack next: find an image strong in one function that is ALSO strong in another (would weaken the functional relativism), and find a genre where the rule misfires.

## Q2/Q3 evidence (professor's questions) — explicit meaning, emotional intelligence
Explicit meaning succeeding: BK-01/BK-02 (both *Attention Seeking Behaviour* covers perform their fully-stated title), MP-03 (torn Gropius), MP-04 (Monet announcement). ## H4 — Quality criteria are functions of the image's job · REFINED (v2) after Type A attack
Attack result: multi-function images exist, exactly where the review predicted. The Jaws and Alien one-sheets are commercial announcements that are simultaneously dread-arguments and remain powerful fifty years out of context at thumbnail scale. Functional boundaries are porous upward.
v2: **Function sets the entry test, not the ceiling.** An image must pass its genre's minimum (the Monet poster must announce; the Jaws poster must sell tickets). The strongest images pass their own genre's test AND one or more others' (announcement + argument + mood). Functional relativism holds for judging adequacy; it does not cap excellence. Misjudgment still comes from applying genre B's test as genre A's minimum.
New function discovered (album covers): the EMBLEM/sigil — an image serving as the durable face of a body of work met repeatedly over years. Its criterion is non-exhaustion (Dark Side of the Moon: a diagram cannot be used up). Criteria catalog so far: announcement → authority/legibility; argument → required participation; emblem → non-exhaustion.

## H5 (new) — Asymmetry, not concealment · PROPOSED
From MV-01/MV-03: Jaws shows everything, Alien shows nothing; both produce dread. The shared engine is an asymmetry between what the viewer knows and what the image's world knows — the viewer placed just before the event, complicit. Concealment is one tool for building asymmetry; full disclosure under a different axis (the swimmer can't see down) is another. Connects to the participation thread: narrative participation = being positioned in time.
Attack next: find strong narrative images with NO asymmetry (symmetrical knowledge) — candidates in children's picture books (Vector D), where complicity with the reader works differently.

## H2 v2 — Required participation · STRENGTHENED by Vector C, taxonomy growing

The strong direct image assigns the viewer a role — conscript (Uncle Sam's second-person grammar), ironist (Pietà/"invest your son"), answerer ("Are we good citizens?"), carrier (Panther badge). ## OUTCOME C (Assignment 5A) — Two engines · PROPOSED, reported on discovery
Participation (task-assignment) is regional, not universal. A second engine — PRESENCE — explains the contemplative family: the image as a record of attention paid, the maker's tempo legible in the artifact, the viewer receiving rather than performing. Evidence: Morandi/Hammershøi/Hasui/Leiter strong with zero task; Leiter dissociates place-from-task; Morandi dissociates both.
Constraints honored: no new participation categories were minted; CB-04 (moonlit monster) now has a candidate home in the presence family but is NOT yet absorbed — held in counterexample log until presence survives its own attack round.
Predicted by presence (falsifiable): (1) imitation-quietness (NR-25 class) should fail despite identical surface vocabulary, because no attention was paid to be recorded; (2) attention-records should survive reproduction at small scale poorly compared to participation images (the tempo lives in facture); (3) commitment finding (drawn-as-if-true) should turn out to be presence's rendering-side twin.
Attack queue for presence: decorative/ornament traditions (professor's predicted strongest threat to commitment), abstraction (Rothko, Agnes Martin), and the question of whether photography can fake recorded attention.

## Null class — second genre confirmed

## H6 (new, from 5D) — Devices transfer; relations don't · PROPOSED
Surface: some strong works feel specific, others swappable. Mechanism: a work's load-bearing element is a RELATION (image-to-subject, image-to-text, image-to-moment); relations break under transfer to neighboring subjects, while devices (style, technique, vocabulary) move freely. Strong works tolerate device-swaps (ASB US/UK; Morandi's own series) and fail relation-swaps (Alien egg→creature; Jaws relation→frontal shark; Economist lawn→headline weld). Emblem-class exception: irreplaceability is deposited by repeated use, not generated internally — consistent with the temporal-contract framework.
Unifies the counterfeit class: all four counterfeit instances are device-bundles with no relation to break — born transferred. (Operational vocabulary only; the forbidden words were not needed, which is itself evidence the observable core was always separable.)
Attack next: find a strong work whose load-bearing element is provably a device, not a relation; find a relation that DOES transfer without loss.

## Counterfeit mechanism (5E) — contract violation · PROPOSED
Counterfeit work advertises one contract and performs another; the felt fakeness is the promise-payment gap, continuous in degree (assertion-without-discovery = mild; templated depth = severe). Subsumes Options A (H6 construction), B (H4 ledger), C (commitment, maker-side). Predicts: honest lightness doesn't feel fake; judges can misfire by misreading the claimed contract (5C swoosh-badge error, corrected).
Standing falsifier: DSOTM — diagram vocabulary, emblem performance, no counterfeit feel. Must be resolved before this mechanism is trusted.
