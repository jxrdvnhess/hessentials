# UTM — Instagram Stories

Append to every Story link.

## Pattern

```
?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=<slug>
```

Replace `<slug>` with the article's URL slug.

## Variants

Other surfaces use the same `utm_source` / `utm_medium` discipline.

| Surface | utm_source | utm_medium |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram Story | instagram | story |
| Instagram bio link | instagram | bio |
| Instagram post caption | instagram | post |
| Newsletter | newsletter | email |
| Friend / DM share | personal | dm |

`utm_campaign` is the article slug (or a date for weekly sends, e.g. `weekly-2026-05-25`).

## Why this matters

Stories link-stickers strip referrers. Without UTM, every Story tap reads as "Direct" in GA — which is currently 66% of all traffic. With UTM in place, Instagram becomes legible and we can tell which articles the audience actually pulls on.
