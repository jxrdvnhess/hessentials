# Brief — Design critique, June 2026

**Status: parked. Agreed in conversation 2026-06-11; not in scope for any current round.**
**Scope when picked up: each item below is its own round. Do not bundle.**

## Premise

The identity is here and coherent. The risk is no longer clutter — it is the schema hardening. The system's own restraint is becoming an accent: pages get laid out the way the system knows, not the way the content needs.

## Flags

### 1. One register, repeated

Almost every element speaks in the identical voice — italic serif whisper, eyebrow tracking, ink at 45–70% opacity, HandRule between sections. A 1990–2005 magazine had registers: front-of-book brisk, feature slow, back page wry. The site has one register at one volume. Monotone-quiet is its own kind of performance.

### 2. Opacity as the universal tool

Nearly all hierarchy is #1f1d1b at some fraction. Nothing is fully present except the headline; everything else pre-announces its own modesty. The copy has teeth; the design renders the teeth at 60% opacity. Candidate move: one element per page in full ink, chosen deliberately.

### 3. Pillar pages lag the doctrine

"One intelligence, many rooms" wants shared grammar plus per-pillar conditions. Currently the four pillars are the same room with different nouns on the door. The template holds — the conditions inside it haven't differentiated. Recipes and Practice should not feel interchangeable at a squint.

### 4. The feather — question once, don't change yet

Edges dissolving into cream is becoming the house move. Covers are historically confident rectangles; the feather is partly a way of not committing to an edge. Decide whether it is chosen each time or defaulted to. Not a veto candidate — an awareness candidate.

### 5. Homepage body is thin — grow, don't pad

After the cover: Aurelian band, Currently, done. Honest given current inventory, so no action now. As the article base grows, the cover earns a real table of contents beneath it. Grow into it; never decorate toward it.

## Governing line

The next phase of authenticity is letting individual pieces of content override the system's defaults — the way real landmarks override the rig.
