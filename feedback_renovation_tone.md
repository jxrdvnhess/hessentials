# feedback_renovation_tone.md

The "under renovation" placeholder works because it is **temporary and honest** — light, imperfect, human, slightly humorous, temporally honest. The honesty is the emotional engine; the illustration is just its current carrier.

Protect that. Do NOT develop it into:
- "the Hessentials illustration system"
- recurring mascots or characters
- whimsical identity language

It works because it feels temporary, not because it's a style. When the renovation is genuinely done, the placeholder **comes down** — it does not evolve, get a sequel, or become permanent furniture. Honesty has an expiration date, and that's the feature.

**Governing principle for all future additions: discoveries, not declarations.** A house becoming inhabited, not a set trying to look inhabited. Each addition should accumulate because it's true — observation, correction, refinement, lived residue — not be declared because it should be impressive. Lived residue passes; atmosphere-for-its-own-sake fails.

Same family as `feedback_teeth_not_decor` and the made-not-captured / honest-fiction line: honesty over performance. This is why the homepage finally feels emotionally honest — the panic is gone, and additions can now be made from inside a coherent structure instead of carrying existential weight.
